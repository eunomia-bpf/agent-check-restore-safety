\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}

Agent runtimes increasingly expose Fork, Restore, and Merge while constructing
their execution graphs online~\cite{codexappserver,claudesessions,wang2026fork,
wu2026crab}.  Unlike ordinary restart, these operations may leave several
descendants live.  Meanwhile tool effects and the receipts that consume their
authority remain outside any restorable workspace.

\paragraph{The failure}
Consider three fresh commitments $a,b,c$.  Their authority contract permits
every singleton and pair, but forbids the triple.  Before a history change,
one joint controller can enforce that correlation.  A parallel Fork, live
Restore, or join Merge may instead leave three controllers independently
authoritative.  Each approves one fresh cell; no receipt is replayed, every
per-effect check passes, and every pair is permitted.  Their product still
realizes the forbidden durable outcome $\{a,b,c\}$.

This is the security effect specific to history-transforming agent execution:
the operation does not merely reconstruct state or retry an action.  It changes
the \emph{co-durability relation} among online-generated continuations without
minting new authority.  The future graph is unavailable when the original
authority is issued, and ordinary agent traces identify calls but not
necessarily the redemption and controller identities that govern their joint
outcomes.

\paragraph{Why adjacent mechanisms do not decide the edge}
Rollback recovery and state continuity protect the past
~\cite{elnozahy2002rollback,parno2011memoir,zheng2026acrfence}.  Linear or
revocable capabilities and commit-time authorization protect one use or one
effect witness~\cite{bowers2007consumable,santos2026lingering,
santos2026committime}.  History monitors enforce policies over supplied
observations~\cite{palumbo2026forge}.  None alone answers whether a proposed
history edge preserves a correlated authority contract when its controllers
can remain live independently.  The question is global even when every effect
is fresh and locally authorized.

\paragraph{History admission}
For each typed edge, we derive the future configurations it may create and
those its semantics requires.  An immutable receipt prefix filters this
candidate future to the authority-admitted family
$\mathsf{Admitted}$.  The submitted controller layout induces
$\mathsf{RawPhysical}$, the configurations its independently live gates can
realize.  We admit the edge exactly when
\begin{equation}
  \mathsf{Required}\subseteq\mathsf{RawPhysical}
  \subseteq\mathsf{Admitted}.
  \label{eq:intro-readiness}
\end{equation}
The first inclusion prevents a vacuous ``safe'' implementation that deletes
promised behavior.  The second rejects the forbidden triple in
\cref{fig:running-failure}.  A deployed runtime additionally establishes
$\mathsf{Required}\subseteq\mathsf{Actual}\subseteq
\mathsf{RawPhysical}$.

\paragraph{Central result: an exact evidence boundary}
Deciding \cref{eq:intro-readiness} requires structural evidence that common
call traces erase.  First, copied handles must be normalized to semantic
redemption cells; second, gate uses must be normalized to authoritative
controller instances.  A mechanized two-by-two construction holds every other
field fixed and shows that erasing either relation conflates a ready and
nonready world.  After both normalizations, a downward-closed co-liveness
family $\Gamma$ records which controllers may remain authoritative together.
The contract derives an arity $r$, and the complete projection
$\Pi_{\leq r}(\Gamma)$ determines readiness exactly.  For every uniformly
smaller bound, a well-formed cover has identical visible projection but the
opposite decision.  The result therefore identifies a contract-indexed
observation depth, not a universal pairwise rule.

\paragraph{Construction and validation}
A certifying compiler implements this decision for typed choice/parallel Fork,
replacing/live Restore, and select/join Merge.  It emits a seal or a minimal
obstruction; an independent verifier recomputes the result.  Greatest
fixed-prefix admission and safe co-liveness pruning are constructive
corollaries, not separate headline claims.  Lean~4 mechanizes the theorem
spine.  The implementation passes 62 tests, and an independent oracle agrees
on all 2,166 bounded refinement instances.  A Codex adapter study locates the
attestation boundary; trajectories demonstrate missing observations but
provide neither authorization nor safety labels.

\noindent\textbf{Contributions.}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt]
  \item We formulate history admission for online Agent Fork/Restore/Merge as
  \cref{eq:intro-readiness}, separating required behavior, durable-prefix
  authority, and physical controller composition.
  \item We prove and mechanize its exact structural evidence boundary: two
  independently nonerasable identity relations plus a sufficient and
  worst-case necessary contract-indexed co-liveness projection.  A certifying
  compiler, independent verifier, oracle, and runtime boundary study validate
  the result and expose its explicit attestation assumptions.
\end{enumerate}
