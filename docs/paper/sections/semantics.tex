\section{Exact History Admission}
\label{sec:admission}

Let \(\mathcal U\) be the authority-label alphabet and
\(\mathcal A\subseteq\mathcal U^*\) a regular,
prefix-closed authority policy.  The authenticated receipt log \(\Delta\)
contains cell identities, phases, and qualified authority labels.  Its
projection \(\mathsf{lab}(\Delta)\in\mathcal A\) is an authority word.  Write
\(D_\Delta\) for its receipted cells.
For a proposed edit, fix the full admission instance
\(\Theta=(\kappa,H,\Delta,\mathcal A,u)\) and its unique schema derivation
\[
 \Sigma_\zeta;H\vdash u\Downarrow
 (H',\mathcal C_{H,u},\eta^-,\eta^+).
\]
Let \(\rho'\) be the authenticated target registry in \(H'\), and define
\(O_{H,u}=O_{\mathcal C_{H,u}}\).  Every language below is a function of
\(\Theta\), the fixed schema registry, and this derived \(\rho'\).  Subscripts
\(H,u\) suppress those parameters only for readability.

\subsection{Fresh and alias resolution}

Resolution never erases an occurrence retained by the derived contract.  Starting
with \(R_0=D_\Delta\), the deterministic transducer
\(\Resolve^{\rho'}_\Delta\) maps a raw word \(w=x_1\cdots x_n\) to a resolved word
\(z_1\cdots z_n\).  For \(d_i=q^{\rho'}_{\rm cell}(x_i)\),
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:resolve}
 z_i=
 \begin{cases}
  \Fresh(x_i,d_i), & d_i\notin R_{i-1},
       \quad R_i=R_{i-1}\cup\{d_i\};\\
  \Alias(x_i,d_i), & d_i\in R_{i-1},
       \quad R_i=R_{i-1}.
 \end{cases}
\end{equation}
The authority projection erases aliases but retains occurrences.
\[
 \mathsf{auth}(\Fresh(x,d))=\ell_{\rho'}(d),\qquad
 \mathsf{auth}(\Alias(x,d))=\epsilon .
\]
For a resolved word \(z\), \(\mathsf{raw}(z)\) erases the fresh/alias mode and
cell fields but retains its ordered logical occurrence identifiers.
A repeated delivery of the \emph{same} invocation identifier is \Retry and
does not occur a second time in \(w\).  In contrast, two restored occurrences
with the same cell both occur in \(w\), although one may be fresh and the other
alias.

\begin{lemma}[Resolution]
\label{lem:resolution}
\(\Resolve^{\rho'}_\Delta\) is deterministic, length preserving, and prefix
compatible.  Its fresh projection contains each cell at most once outside
\(D_\Delta\), while its occurrence projection is exactly the input word.
Moreover, if
\(\Resolve^{\rho'}_\Delta(rv)=zv'\), then
\(z=\Resolve^{\rho'}_\Delta(r)\) and
\(v'=\Resolve^{\rho'}_{\Delta_z}(v)\), where \(\Delta_z\) extends \(\Delta\) by the
fresh events of \(z\).
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Induct on the input length and the accumulated set \(R_i\).  Each step emits
one symbol, updates \(R_i\) only in the fresh case, and depends only on the
input prefix.  Set membership gives uniqueness of fresh emission.  Both cases
retain \(x_i\).  Splitting the same induction after \(r\) gives the streaming
equation.
\end{proof}

This lemma fixes two distinct replay cases.  A network retry is a stutter in
both histories.  A copied occurrence is a stutter only in the authority
projection because it still resolves choice, sequence, or join causality.

\subsection{Prefix-robust history admission}

For each structurally derived outcome \(o\in O_{H,u}\), define its set of
resolved completions and the corresponding safe subset.
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:safe-completions}
\begin{aligned}
 R_o(H,u)&=\{\Resolve^{\rho'}_\Delta(w)\mid w\in\Lin(p_o)\},\\
 M_o(H,u)&=\{z\in R_o(H,u)\mid
   \mathsf{lab}(\Delta)\mathsf{auth}(z)\in\mathcal A\}.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
The two definitions now share an alphabet.  \(M_o\) contains resolved
occurrence words, and the policy is consulted only through their authority
projection.

Initial outcome coverage is insufficient.  One safe schedule for outcome
\(o\) and another for \(v\) may share a prefix that makes \(o\)'s remaining
schedule unsafe, silently pruning an outcome that the raw contract still
retains.  We therefore close safe completions under every shared prefix.
For a resolved prefix \(z\), let
\[
 \mathsf{Compat}(z)=
 \{o\mid \mathsf{raw}(z)\preceq_{\rm pref}w
          \text{ for some }w\in\Lin(p_o)\}.
\]
Identical resolved words may belong to different indexed outcomes, so the
fixed point must not quotient their identities.  Put
\[
 \widehat B_0=\biguplus_{o\in O_{H,u}}\{o\}\times M_o .
\]
For \(\widehat B\subseteq\widehat B_0\), define the monotone operator
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:robust-operator}
\widehat\Phi(\widehat B)=
\left\{(o,b)\in\widehat B_0\ \middle|\
\begin{array}{l}
\forall z\preceq_{\rm pref} b.\ \forall v\in\mathsf{Compat}(z).\\[-2pt]
\exists (v,b')\in\widehat B.\ z\preceq_{\rm pref}b'
\end{array}\right\}.
\end{equation}
Set \(\widehat B_{i+1}=\widehat\Phi(\widehat B_i)\).
Because \(\widehat B_1\subseteq\widehat B_0\), and monotonicity gives
\(\widehat B_{i+1}\subseteq\widehat B_i\) whenever
\(\widehat B_i\subseteq\widehat B_{i-1}\), the resulting finite sequence
descends and stabilizes.  Let
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \widehat B^\dagger_{H,u}
   &=\nu\widehat B.\widehat\Phi(\widehat B)
     =\bigcap_{i\geq 0}\widehat B_i,\\
 B^\dagger_{H,u}&=\pi_2\widehat B^\dagger_{H,u},
 &W^\dagger_{H,u}&=\Pref(B^\dagger_{H,u}).
\end{aligned}
\]
The fixed point removes a completion if one of its prefixes lacks a surviving
completion carrying even one still-compatible outcome index.  This removal can
invalidate coverage at an earlier prefix.  The runtime consumes the projected
marked language \(B^\dagger\), while the checker and seal retain
\(\widehat B^\dagger\) as a ghost witness relation.

\begin{definition}[Agent history admission]
\label{def:admission}
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:admission}
\mathsf{Admit}(H,u,\Delta,\mathcal A)
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \widehat B^\dagger_{H,u}\ne\varnothing .
\end{equation}
\end{definition}
We write \(\mathsf{Admit}(H,u)\) only after the full instance \(\Theta\),
schema registry, and derived target registry have been fixed.

At \(z=\epsilon\), every derived outcome is compatible.  Thus nonemptiness of
the fixed point already implies initial outcome coverage.  The inner
existential chooses a scheduler linearization rather than promising fairness.
The outer prefix condition prevents that choice from silently deleting another
outcome later.

\begin{definition}[Indexed realization]
\label{def:realization}
A generated language and indexed marked family
\((L,\widehat B)\) realize \((H,u)\) when:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=1pt]
 \item \(\widehat B\ne\varnothing\);
 \item \emph{history fidelity:}
\(\widehat B\subseteq
 \biguplus_{o\in O_{H,u}}\{o\}\times R_o(H,u)\) and
\(L\subseteq\Pref(\bigcup_{o\in O_{H,u}}R_o(H,u))\);
 \item \emph{policy safety:}
 \(\mathsf{lab}(\Delta)\,\mathsf{auth}(z)\in\mathcal A\)
 for every \(z\in L\);
 \item \emph{completion nonblocking:}
 \(L=\Pref(\pi_2\widehat B)\); and
 \item \emph{persistent outcome coverage:} for every \(z\in L\) and
 \(o\in\mathsf{Compat}(z)\), some
 \((o,b)\in\widehat B\) extends \(z\).
\end{enumerate}
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Prefix-robust greatest realization]
\label{lem:greatest-realization}
A realization exists iff \(\mathsf{Admit}(H,u)\).
On admission,
\((W^\dagger_{H,u},\widehat B^\dagger_{H,u})\) is a realization, and every
realization \((L,\widehat B)\) satisfies
\(\widehat B\subseteq\widehat B^\dagger_{H,u}\) and
\(L\subseteq W^\dagger_{H,u}\).
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Fidelity, completion nonblocking, and safety put the indexed marked family of
any realization inside \(\widehat B_0\).  Persistent coverage says exactly that
\(\widehat B\subseteq\widehat\Phi(\widehat B)\), so \(\widehat B\) is a
post-fixed point.  Monotonicity of \(\widehat\Phi\) and the finite
Knaster--Tarski construction place every such family inside
\(\widehat B^\dagger\).  Taking projected prefixes
gives \(L\subseteq W^\dagger\).  Conversely,
\(\widehat B^\dagger=\widehat\Phi(\widehat B^\dagger)\) supplies persistent
coverage, \(\widehat B^\dagger\subseteq\widehat B_0\) supplies fidelity and
safe marked words.  Prefix closure of \(\mathcal A\) supplies safety for their
prefixes, while \(W^\dagger=\Pref(\pi_2\widehat B^\dagger)\) supplies
nonblocking.  The fixed point is a realization exactly when nonempty.
\end{proof}

This fixed point is substantive even when every \(M_o\) is nonempty.  Let
\(X,Y,Z\) be singleton contracts over distinct fresh cells labeled
\(x,y,z\), respectively.  Let \(\mathcal C=X\otimes(Y\oplus Z)\) and
\(\mathcal A=\Pref\{yx,xz\}\).  The two outcomes initially have safe
completions \(yx\) and \(xz\).  Yet prefix \(x\), taken from \(xz\), remains
raw-compatible with the \(xy\) outcome and leaves it no safe continuation.
The first iteration removes \(xz\).  The next removes \(yx\) because root
coverage is then lost.  Hence the edit is rejected, whereas the independent
\(\forall o.\,M_o\ne\varnothing\) test would admit it.

Rejection has a finite proof.  For every
\((o_{\rm rem},b)\in\widehat B_i\setminus\widehat B_{i+1}\), record a prefix
\(z\preceq b\) and outcome
\(o_{\rm miss}\in\mathsf{Compat}(z)\) for which no
\((o_{\rm miss},b')\in\widehat B_i\) extends \(z\).  The removal round is its
rank.
For any post-fixed family \(P\), forward induction gives
\(P\subseteq\widehat B_i\).  The base is \(\widehat B_0\), and each recorded
missing-witness cause excludes its removed pair from \(P\), yielding
\(P\subseteq\widehat B_{i+1}\).  If the sequence reaches \(\varnothing\), no
nonempty post-fixed family, hence no realization, exists.  The checker
recomputes \(\widehat B_0\), verifies nestedness and a cause for every removed
pair, and checks every survivor coverage witness at stabilization.

To preserve admission after each permitted prefix, fix \(H,u,\mathcal A\)
after deriving \(\mathcal C\), and write
\(W^\dagger(\mathcal C,\Delta)\) and
\(\widehat B^\dagger(\mathcal C,\Delta)\) with those parameters suppressed.
\begin{lemma}[Residual coherence]
\label{lem:residual-coherence}
If \(z\in W^\dagger(\mathcal C,\Delta)\), then
\(\Resolve^{\rho'}_\Delta(\mathsf{raw}(z))=z\),
\(\mathsf{lab}(\Delta_z)=
 \mathsf{lab}(\Delta)\mathsf{auth}(z)\), and
\[
\begin{aligned}
 W^\dagger(\mathcal C,\Delta)/z
  &=W^\dagger(\mathcal C/\mathsf{raw}(z),\Delta_z),\\
 \widehat B^\dagger(\mathcal C,\Delta)/z
  &=\widehat B^\dagger(
      \mathcal C/\mathsf{raw}(z),\Delta_z),
\end{aligned}
\]
where
\(\widehat B/z=\{(o,v)\mid(o,zv)\in\widehat B\}\).
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Because \(z\) prefixes the projection of a member of
\(\widehat B^\dagger\), prefix compatibility and raw
recoverability in \cref{lem:resolution} give the first equality.  Its streaming
case gives the ledger equality.  Taking the indexed pomset residual removes
exactly that raw prefix while retaining every consistent outcome identity.
Moreover,
\(\mathsf{Compat}_{\mathcal C/\mathsf{raw}(z)}(s)
 =\mathsf{Compat}_{\mathcal C}(zs)\).
Streaming resolution and authority concatenation give the load-bearing base
equality
\[
 \widehat B_0(\mathcal C,\Delta)/z
 =\widehat B_0(\mathcal C/\mathsf{raw}(z),\Delta_z),
\]
with the same retained outcome identities, policy, and target registry.
The quotient \(\widehat B^\dagger/z\) is therefore a post-fixed family for
the residual operator, hence is contained in its greatest fixed point \(Y\).
Conversely, let \(zY=\{(o,zy)\mid(o,y)\in Y\}\).
The streaming equation and residual policy safety put \(zY\) inside the
original \(\widehat B_0\).  Then
\(\widehat B^\dagger\cup zY\) is post-fixed for the original operator.
Prefixes \(t\preceq z\) use witnesses already supplied by
\(\widehat B^\dagger\), while prefixes \(t=zs\) use residual witnesses in \(Y\).
Maximality of \(\widehat B^\dagger\) gives
\(zY\subseteq\widehat B^\dagger\), so
\(Y=\widehat B^\dagger/z\).  Finally,
\(\Pref(B)/z=\Pref(B/z)\) for \(z\in\Pref(B)\).  Taking projected prefixes
gives the generated equality.
\end{proof}
Thus every admitted prefix retains a safe completion for every residual
outcome index still structurally consistent with that prefix.

\subsection{The ideal atomic history machine}
\label{sec:ideal}

The ideal LTS retains abstract gate and epoch identities so protected calls
and history edges are defined, but has no phase protocol, seals, preloading,
or handoff microsteps.  A history edge changes the policy-domain epoch
atomically.
Its durable state contains \((\kappa,H,\Delta,\mathsf{out})\) plus
the current admitted cut
\(c=(H_c,u,W^\dagger_{H_c,u},\widehat B^\dagger_{H_c,u})\) and resolved prefix
\(r\).  It has the following transitions.

\paragraph{History edge}
Let \(H_c=H\) in the pre-edge state.  \(\mathsf{Edge}(u)\) atomically applies the unique judgment
\(\Sigma_\zeta;H\vdash u\Downarrow(H',\mathcal C,\eta^-,\eta^+)\) when
\(\mathsf{Admit}(H,u)\), persists the already version-advanced \(H'\), sets
\(c:=(H_c,u,W^\dagger_{H_c,u},\widehat B^\dagger_{H_c,u})\), and sets
\(r:=\epsilon\).  No semantic handoff
protocol is present.  This independent atomic behavior is what the
implementation must refine.

\paragraph{Protected occurrence}
Let \(x\) be an enabled occurrence of the current frontier with expected gate
and authenticated cell \(d\).  Resolve it against the current receipts to
\(a\in\{\Fresh(x,d),\Alias(x,d)\}\).  The transition is enabled exactly when
\(ra\in W^\dagger_{H_c,u}\).  It atomically residualizes the frontier by \(x\),
appends \(a\) to \(\chi\), advances \(\nu\), and sets
\(r:=ra\).  In the fresh case it also appends an immutable receipt
for \(d\), labeled \(\ell(d)\), to \(\Delta\) and enqueues \(d\).  In the alias
case those fields do not change.

Failure of any protected-occurrence guard, including an invalid occurrence,
gate, or handle, a disabled branch, or
\(ra\notin W^\dagger_{H_c,u}\), produces \(\mathsf{deny}(x)\) without
changing state.  An alias binds the restored
occurrence to the same durable result future as its cell.  It advances causal
history but does not invent a result that the original invocation has not yet
produced.

\paragraph{Retry, release, and crash}
An authenticated same-invocation retry follows its \(\chi\)-to-receipt link
and returns the stable future/result without changing history.  An
unauthenticated lookup is denied.
\Dispatch may release only the oldest prepared outbox item and marks that
entry released, so the release trace is a prefix of fresh preparation order.
Settlement monotonically advances its receipt phase and fills the stable
future/result, but changes no history or authority field.  Crash discards
nondurable computation and leaves the LTS state unchanged.  We make no claim
about completion order inside an external network after release.

By \cref{lem:greatest-realization}, every reachable reference prefix retains
a policy-safe completion until the next explicit history edge changes the
contract.  The next sections implement this ideal machine without consulting
the full completion set at every call.
