\section{Certificate-Checked Lifecycle Semantics}
\label{sec:semantics}

The durable controller is the least labeled transition relation generated by the rules below:
\[
A\xrightarrow{u/\eta}A',
\qquad
\eta::=\tau\mid\mathsf{attempt}(e,c).
\]
An attempt label releases stable operation ID \(e\) and claim \(c\) toward a protected sink; retries may produce several attempts with the same pair.
The generated operations are Checkpoint, Reserve, choice/parallel Fork,
Select, Abort, replace/live Restore, Merge, Prepare, Dispatch, Retry, Crash,
Settle, and Revoke; arguments and certificates appear in their rules.
Every rule reads one durable pre-state and crash-atomically installs its listed update.
All arithmetic is checked natural-number arithmetic.

For a claim \(c\), let \(\nu(c)\) be its immutable grant epoch, and let \(L(A)=\bigcup_{b\in B}Q_b\).
For \(c\in L(A)\), \(\mathsf{open}_A(c)\) means that both \(\mathsf{own}_A(c)\) and \(\nu(c)\) are open in \(N\).
Contract restriction is the canonical syntax operation
\(\mathsf{Restrict}((P,\Gamma),S)=(P|_S,\Gamma|_{2^S})\), where \(P|_S\) recursively replaces every leaf outside \(S\) by \(\mathbf0\).
Write it as \(T\mathbin{\upharpoonright}S\); by construction,
\[
\front(T\mathbin{\upharpoonright}S)
=\{C\in\front(T)\mid C\subseteq S\}.
\]
The deterministic function \(\mathsf{clean}\) closes every unsupported owner with a nonempty tentative bundle, moves that bundle to \(X\), and restricts the contract accordingly; it returns the removed owners and support witnesses for all remaining ones.

\subsection{Topology certificates}

A topology target descriptor is \(\Delta=(B',N',Q',T',\rho)\).
Write \(L'=\bigcup_{b\in B'}Q'_b\); it builds
\[
A[\Delta]=(G,N',B',D,X\cup(L(A)\setminus L'),Q',T',J,R).
\]
Thus a topology step preserves \(G,D,J,R\) exactly and terminalizes every old tentative ID not literally retained.
The transfer certificate is a total map
\(\rho:L'\to L(A)\), and \(L'\cap(D\cup X)=\varnothing\).
For each source \(c\), the fiber \(\rho^{-1}(c)\) contains either the unchanged ID \(c\), with immutable metadata, or globally fresh issuer-approved fragments---never both.
Fiber members have compatible refined bindings and
\(\sum_{c'\in\rho^{-1}(c)}w(c')\le w(c)\).
This permits certified divisible splitting, but not copying.

Let \(\mathsf{Chk}(p,F)\) be a sound checker for a finite proof object \(p\) and closed pseudo-Boolean formula \(F\); completeness is unnecessary.
The certificate datatype is
\[
\begin{aligned}
\kappa::={}&\mathsf{res}(p)\mid
\mathsf{adm}(\Delta,\omega,p)\mid\\[-1mm]
&\mathsf{sim}(\Delta,\pi,\omega,p)\mid
\mathsf{seal}(\gamma,Z,\omega,\bar p).
\end{aligned}
\]
Here \(\omega(b)\) is a concrete support witness, and \(\bar p(z)\) proves that cleanup owner \(z\) has none.
The predicate \(\mathsf{WFDelta}_A(\Delta,\omega)\) checks that the target bundles \(Q'_b\) are pairwise disjoint, \(\rho\)'s domain is exactly \(L'\), and the disjointness, fiber conservation, freshness, and binding conditions above hold; it also checks monotone epoch closure, freshness of opened epochs, contract well-formedness,
that \(B'\) is exactly the set of branch-typed epochs open in \(N'\),
\(N'(\nu(c))=\mathsf{open}\) for every \(c\in L'\), and
\[
Q'_b\ne\varnothing\Longrightarrow b\in\omega(b)\in\front(T').
\]
A simulation certificate additionally supplies a monotone, zero-preserving \(\pi:2^{B'}\to2^B\) and proves
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\forall C'\in\front(T').\quad&
\pi(C')\in\front(T),\\[-1mm]
&d+q'(C')\le d+q(\pi(C')).
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:simulation}
\end{equation}
Call this closed formula \(\mathsf{Sim}_{A,\Delta,\pi}\).
A direct-admission certificate instead proves
\begin{equation}
\forall C'\in\front(T').\quad d+q'(C')\le G.
\label{eq:direct-admission}
\end{equation}
Call this closed formula \(\mathsf{Adm}_{A,\Delta}\).
Writing \(\mathsf{Mono}_0(\pi)\) for monotonicity and zero preservation, let
\(\kappa_s=\mathsf{sim}(\Delta,\pi,\omega,p)\) and
\(\kappa_a=\mathsf{adm}(\Delta,\omega,p)\), and define
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathsf{SimOK}_A(\kappa_s)
&\Longleftrightarrow \mathsf{WFDelta}_A(\Delta,\omega)
 \land\mathsf{Mono}_0(\pi)\\[-1mm]
&\hspace{17mm}\land\mathsf{Chk}(p,\mathsf{Sim}_{A,\Delta,\pi}),\\
\mathsf{AdmOK}_A(\kappa_a)
&\Longleftrightarrow \mathsf{WFDelta}_A(\Delta,\omega)
 \land\mathsf{Chk}(p,\mathsf{Adm}_{A,\Delta}).
\end{aligned}
\]
For base contracts these formulas have structural derivations.
For guarded contracts a proof may be produced by the global oracle; the small checker, rather than the untrusted adapter, is in the trusted base.
In a simulation-certified topology step, old predicates are composed with \(\pi\), and the resulting circuit and proof sizes are counted in the certificate.

For \(T=(E[b],\Gamma)\), define the canonical projection
\[
\pi_{S\to b}(C')=(C'\setminus S)\cup
\begin{cases}\{b\},&C'\cap S\ne\varnothing,\\
\varnothing,&C'\cap S=\varnothing.
\end{cases}
\]
The finite predicate \(\mathsf{Shape}_u(A,\Delta,\pi)\) holds exactly in the following cases.
For choice or parallel Fork, fresh \(b_0,b_1\) give
\(B'=(B\setminus\{b\})\cup\{b_0,b_1\}\),
\(T'=(E[b_0\mathbin\odot b_1],\pi^*\Gamma)\) for
\(\odot\in\{\Box,\parallel\}\), and
\(\pi=\pi_{\{b_0,b_1\}\to b}\); \(N'\) closes \(b\), opens the two fresh epochs, and otherwise agrees with \(N\).
For replace Restore, fresh \(b'\) gives
\(B'=(B\setminus\{b\})\cup\{b'\}\),
\(T'=(E[b'],\pi^*\Gamma)\), and
\(\pi=\pi_{\{b'\}\to b}\); \(N'\) closes \(b\), opens \(b'\), and otherwise agrees with \(N\).
For live Restore, fresh \(b'\) gives
\(B'=B\cup\{b'\}\),
\(T'=(E[b\parallel b'],\pi^*\Gamma)\), and
\(\pi=\pi_{\{b,b'\}\to b}\); \(N'\) keeps \(b\) open, opens \(b'\), and otherwise agrees with \(N\).
Thus every transported singleton lineage test uses OR over its descendants.
Let \(\mathsf{Shape}_{\mathsf{merge}}(A,\Delta)\) hold exactly when finite
\(B',N',Q',T',\rho\) are explicit, every eliminated live branch epoch is closed, no closed epoch is reopened, and every newly opened epoch is fresh.
The remaining case of \(\mathsf{Shape}_u(A,\Delta,\pi)\) is a simulation-certified Merge with this lifecycle shape and an explicit \(\pi\); hence \(u\) ranges over exactly the four Fork/Restore forms above and Merge.
Fork and restore require \(\kappa_s\); merge may use \(\kappa_s\) or instead \(\kappa_a\).
The two rules are
\begin{equation}
\frac{\mathsf{Shape}_u(A,\Delta,\pi)\quad
      \mathsf{SimOK}_A(\kappa_s)}
 {A\xrightarrow{u(\kappa_s)/\tau}A[\Delta]},
\label{rule:struct}
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
\frac{\mathsf{Shape}_{\mathsf{merge}}(A,\Delta)\quad
      \mathsf{WFDelta}_A(\Delta,\omega)\quad
      \mathsf{AdmOK}_A(\kappa_a)}
 {A\xrightarrow{\mathsf{merge}(\kappa_a)/\tau}A[\Delta]}.
\label{rule:merge-adm}
\end{equation}
Checkpointing or reconstructing \(V\) uses
\(A\xrightarrow{\mathsf{checkpoint}/\tau}A\).
The authority calculus places no additional constraint on combining non-authority-bearing \(V\); an authority-bearing merge must pass one of the two rules above.

\subsection{Reservation, restriction, and sealing}

Let \(A+c@b\) add \(c\) to \(Q_b\).
For \(\kappa_r=\mathsf{res}(p)\), fresh Reserve is
\begin{equation}
\frac{\begin{array}{c}
c\notin D\cup X\cup L(A)\quad b\in B\\
N(b)=N(\nu(c))=\mathsf{open}\quad\mathsf{BindOK}(c)\quad
\mathsf{supp}_T(b)\ne\varnothing\\
\mathsf{Chk}(p,\ac(A+c@b))
\end{array}}
 {A\xrightarrow{\mathsf{reserve}(b,c;\kappa_r)/\tau}A+c@b}.
\label{rule:reserve}
\end{equation}
For \(S\subseteq B\), define
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
A\downarrow S=
(&G,N[B\setminus S:=\mathsf{closed}],S,D,\\[-1mm]
 &X\cup\!\bigcup_{b\notin S}Q_b,Q|_S,
 T\mathbin{\upharpoonright}S,J,R).
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
For \(S\in\front(T)\), Select is
\(A\xrightarrow{\mathsf{select}(S)/\tau}A\downarrow S\);
for \(b\in B\), Abort is
\(A\xrightarrow{\mathsf{abort}(b)/\tau}A\downarrow(B\setminus\{b\})\).
Neither operation erases a ticket or refunds a durable claim.

Let \(\varnothing\ne U\subseteq L(A)\) be a fixed promotion batch and \(\bar e:U\to E\) an injective stable-ID binding.
Define
\begin{equation}
a_U=d+\sum_{c\in U}w(c),
\qquad
u_U(b)=r_b-\sum_{c\in U\cap Q_b}w(c),
\label{eq:promotion-load}
\end{equation}
and the uniquely determined frozen row
\begin{equation}
\gamma_U(C)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
a_U+\sum_{b\in C}u_U(b)\le G.
\label{eq:promotion-guard}
\end{equation}
Set \(D^0=D\cup U\), \(Q^0=Q\setminus U\), \(T^0=T\wedge\gamma_U\), and let
\[
Z=\{b\in B\mid Q^0_b\ne\varnothing\land
                 \mathsf{supp}_{T^0}(b)=\varnothing\}.
\]
The predicate \(\mathsf{SealOK}\) recomputes \(\gamma_U\), checks a witness for every continuing nonempty bundle, and checks \(\bar p(z)\) against
\(\neg\exists C\in\front(T^0).\ z\in C\) for every \(z\in Z\).
For \(J_U=J\cup\{\bar e(c)\mapsto(c,\mathsf{prepared})\mid c\in U\}\), define the exact target
\[
\begin{aligned}
A_U=(&G,N[Z:=\mathsf{closed}],B\setminus Z,D^0,\\[-1mm]
&X\cup\!\bigcup_{z\in Z}Q^0_z,\ Q^0|_{B\setminus Z},\\[-1mm]
&T^0\mathbin{\upharpoonright}(B\setminus Z),J_U,R).
\end{aligned}
\]
Set \(\kappa_U=\mathsf{seal}(\gamma_U,Z,\omega,\bar p)\) and let
\(\mathsf{FreshE}(\bar e)\) mean that \(\bar e\) is injective and its range is disjoint from \(\operatorname{dom}J\cup\operatorname{dom}R\).
The complete Prepare rule is
\begin{equation}
\frac{\begin{array}{c}
\forall c\in U.\ \mathsf{open}_A(c)\land
\mathsf{BindOK}(c,\bar e(c))\\
\mathsf{FreshE}(\bar e)\quad a_U\le G\quad
\mathsf{SealOK}_A(U,\bar e,\kappa_U)
\end{array}}
{A\xrightarrow{\mathsf{prepare}(U,\bar e;\kappa_U)/\tau}A_U}.
\label{rule:prepare}
\end{equation}
The installed row retains exactly the old configurations solvent after promotion; cleanup is therefore explicit.
If the runtime requires order-independent dispatch of several owner groups, \cref{thm:serial} decides whether serial Prepare is sufficient; otherwise it must choose and validate an order, defer cleanup under coordination, or seal the fixed batch atomically before dispatch.

For a grant epoch \(\nu\), let
\(Y_\nu=\{c\in L(A)\mid\nu(c)=\nu\}\) and
\[
A\setminus\nu=
A[N(\nu):=\mathsf{closed},Q:=Q\setminus Y_\nu,
X:=X\cup Y_\nu].
\]
Revoke is
\begin{equation}
\frac{N(\nu)=\mathsf{open}}
{A\xrightarrow{\mathsf{revoke}(\nu)/\tau}A\setminus\nu}.
\label{rule:revoke}
\end{equation}
It leaves \(D,J,R\) unchanged, so it blocks new Reserve and Prepare without erasing an already sealed operation.

\subsection{Crash-aware protected dispatch}

Let \(\mathcal S=\{\mathsf{inflight},\mathsf{uncertain}\}\).
Write \(\eta_{e,c}=\mathsf{attempt}(e,c)\);
\(A[e:s]\) updates only \(J(e)\)'s phase to \(s\), and
\(A[e\mapsto(c,o)]\) removes \(J(e)\) and records \(R(e)=(c,o)\).
The remaining rules are
\begin{align}
\frac{J(e)=(c,\mathsf{prepared})\quad c\in D}
 {A\xrightarrow{\mathsf{dispatch}(e)/\eta_{e,c}}
  A[e:\mathsf{inflight}]}
&\label{rule:dispatch}\\
\frac{J(e)=(c,s)\quad s\in\mathcal S\quad c\in D}
 {A\xrightarrow{\mathsf{retry}(e)/\eta_{e,c}}
  A[e:\mathsf{inflight}]}
&\label{rule:retry}\\
A\xrightarrow{\mathsf{crash}/\tau}
A[J:\mathsf{inflight}\mapsto\mathsf{uncertain}]
&\label{rule:crash}\\
\frac{J(e)=(c,s)\quad
[s\in\mathcal S\ \lor\ (s=\mathsf{prepared}\land o=\mathsf{cancelled})]}
 {A\xrightarrow{\mathsf{settle}(e,o)/\tau}
  A[e\mapsto(c,o)]}
&\label{rule:settle}
\end{align}
The Dispatch phase update is durable before the gate performs the represented attempt.
A crash in between is safely over-approximated by an attempt with zero actual outcome.
Every retry reuses the same \((e,c)\).
The sink contract requires either stable-ID deduplication or that the aggregate actual outcome of every physical attempt carrying \(e\) is bounded by \(w(c)\).
Settle never returns \(c\) to \(Q\) or removes it from \(D\); the rules prove safety, not authority reclamation or liveness.
