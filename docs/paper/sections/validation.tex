\section{Executable Validation}
% 可执行验证
\label{sec:validation}

The outcome-indexed bounded validator fails closed beyond its bounds; 81 tests cover shared-prefix rejection, six edits, identity erasures, \Alias, and use/install races.
% 保持结果索引的有界验证器在超出其界限时以关闭方式失败；81 项测试覆盖共享前缀拒绝、六种编辑、标识擦除、\Alias 和使用/安装竞态。
Kernel-checked Lean proves exact acceptance and greatestness for every finite registered resolver/fixed-point instance; six-edit closure, lower bounds, and durable bisimulation remain mathematical proofs.
% 由内核检查的 Lean 证明每个有限已注册解析器/不动点实例满足精确接纳与最大性；六种编辑闭包、下界和持久弱互模拟仍由数学证明给出。
