\section{Executable Validation}
% 可执行验证
\label{sec:validation}

Five Lean modules pass direct \texttt{--trust=0} checking, with exact mapping in \cref{app:lean-map}; factor lower bounds, general pomset lifting, and durable bisimulation remain mathematical proofs.
% 五个 Lean 模块通过直接 \texttt{--trust=0} 检查，精确映射见 \cref{app:lean-map}；因子下界、一般偏序事件集提升与持久互模拟仍由数学证明给出。
Eighty-one tests exercise critical cases; five-process medians are \(53.47\), \(5.51\), and \(50.35\) ms for 128-outcome admission, 128-outcome rejection, and 720 completions, respectively; inputs above stated bounds are rejected before search, and these are decision-kernel rather than Agent-runtime measurements.
% 八十一项测试运行关键案例；五进程耗时中位数分别为 \(53.47\)、\(5.51\) 与 \(50.35\) ms，对应 128 个结果的准入、128 个结果的拒绝与 720 条完成路径；超过既定边界的输入会在搜索前被拒绝，且这些数据属于判定内核而非 Agent 运行时测量。
