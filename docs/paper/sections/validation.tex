\section{Evaluation}
\label{sec:validation}

The evaluation is intentionally subordinate to the theory.  It asks three
questions: which parts of the history-admission characterization are
machine-checked (EQ1), whether two executable implementations agree with the
finite semantics (EQ2), and whether the predicted information losses and
coordination failures appear as concrete witnesses (EQ3).  It does not claim
that an existing Agent runtime satisfies the edge-cut refinement.

\subsection{EQ1: Mechanized theorem components}

Lean~4 checks the finite components from which
\cref{thm:agent-history-admission} is assembled:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt]
  \item the choice, tensor, and replacement family constructors used by the
  six typed edges, including well-formedness and
  $\mathsf{Req}_e\subseteq\mathsf{Cand}_e$;
  \item the durable-prefix morphism, its universal additive-policy
  characterization, and greatest admitted future;
  \item controller-cover deployment readiness, obstruction extraction,
  greatest fixed-manifest co-liveness restriction, and the exact
  contract-indexed projection theorem;
  \item independent cell/controller identity separation and the
  $U(k,k{+}1)$ lower-bound family; and
  \item monotone commitment receipts and compositional structural transport.
\end{itemize}

The audit rejects placeholders, project axioms, and dependencies beyond
\texttt{propext}, \texttt{Quot.sound}, and
\texttt{Classical.choice}.  The reference-monitor LTS equality, the
before/after history-to-effect lift, and the bidirectional connection between a
real installed controller epoch and the manifest language are currently
paper proofs or explicit refinement premises, not one extracted end-to-end
Lean theorem.  We state that boundary in
\cref{tab:proof-boundary}; ``mechanized components'' does not mean verified
Codex or Claude enforcement.

\subsection{EQ2: Compiler--verifier agreement}

The dependency-free Python artifact contains two semantic implementations.
The compiler represents configurations as symbolic cell sets; the independent
verifier reparses the original request and reconstructs the semantics with bit
masks.  Both derive the typed candidate and required families, the admitted
monitor language, the contract arity, and the physical controller language.
The verifier compares canonical results rather than importing compiler code.
It rejects duplicate keys, unknown fields, inconsistent aliases, malformed
receipt bindings, phase regressions, undeclared controller reuse, tampering,
and resource-cap violations.

Of 62 tests, 38 focus on the six history labels, evidence modes,
monitor-language filtering, readiness grades, proposed restrictions,
obstructions, malformed inputs, determinism, and tampering.  Every positive
certificate sets
$\mathsf{effect\_authorizes}=\mathsf{false}$: installation and individual
effect release remain separate runtime acts.

An exhaustive oracle enumerates all 19 downward-closed families over three
cells.  Across every full-support candidate, required subfamily, and physical
family, it checks 2,166 instances of
\[
  \mathsf{Req}_e\subseteq\mathsf{RawPhysical}(E,L,\Gamma)
  \subseteq\mathsf{Adm}_e(D).
\]
The compiler's five diagnostic grades agree with the direct set differences
in every instance, and the independent verifier accepts every untampered
result.  This cross-checks the finite algorithms; it is not a proof that the
JSON parser refines Lean or that a runtime manifest is complete.

A deterministic scaling characterization uses threshold families through
12 cells.  With six cells and one through four controllers, exact full-family
expansion grows from 64 to 70,592 pair iterations.  On a six-cell contract
whose decisive arity is one, projected checking uses 128--640 iterations for
two through ten controllers, whereas explicit full expansion requires
4,288--16,846,784 and exceeds the 200,000 fail-closed cap at five controllers.
These are operation counts, not latency or deployment claims.

\subsection{EQ3: Separating witnesses}

The litmus suite instantiates the lower bounds in
\cref{thm:agent-history-admission}.  A snapshot-identical
\textsc{RestoreReplace}/\textsc{RestoreLive} pair receives opposite verdicts
because only the latter retains the old controller.  A receipt-free and a
receipt-grown copy of the same Restore request separate durable-frontier
evidence.  The $2\times2$ identity fixture keeps all remaining fields fixed:
folding copied handles to one redemption cell removes a replay, while folding
gate uses to one authoritative controller removes a forbidden product; erasing
either quotient conflates an accepted and rejected instance.

For higher-order correlation, every pair of $a,b,c$ is monitor-legal but the
triple is not.  Three independent locally safe controllers therefore produce
a \textsc{CorrelationCut}; a shared controller or the synthesized pairwise
co-liveness restriction passes after resubmission.  Generalizing the fixture,
the $U(k,k{+}1)$ family agrees on every observation of arity at most $k$ but
differs on the $(k{+}1)$-way verdict.  The executable witnesses match the
mechanized constructions and show why edge mode, receipts, both identities,
and contract-indexed co-liveness are theorem inputs rather than logging
conveniences.
