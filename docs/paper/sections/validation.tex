\section{Executable Validation}
\label{sec:validation}

The main result is a pen-and-paper theorem.  The dependency-free artifact is
only a bounded bug finder.

\paragraph{Executable core}
The exact-admission API takes a derived finite indexed-pomset contract,
enumerates its topological sorts, performs length-preserving fresh/alias
resolution, checks authority projections, iterates \(\widehat\Phi\) from
\(\widehat B_0\), and records the chain and ranked removal causes.  On
acceptance, the checker verifies every
survivor/prefix/outcome witness and constructs \(W^\dagger\) exactly.  Outcome
indices remain distinct under equal trace projections, and contract
construction enforces completion observability.  Search fails closed above 128 outcomes, eight
occurrences per outcome, 50,000 linearizations per pomset, or 100,000 safe
indexed completions.  These caps do not bound the theorem.

A separate fixture derives all six edits and rechecks its cut.  It omits
immutable schemas, \(\preccurlyeq_\mu\), retirement, policy-domain epochs, the
canonical program, weak bisimulation, and the complete \Use rule.  It merges
identical pomsets and uses cell IDs as labels.  The paper defines and proves
the omitted layers.  Both representation choices are fixture simplifications.
Production assumes trusted registries, mediation, and durable storage.

\paragraph{Litmus results}
Nineteen focused tests cover (1) the rejecting \(2,1,0\) chain for
\(X\otimes(Y\oplus Z)\) under \(\Pref\{yx,xz\}\), (2) distinct identical-trace
indices, (3) survivor prefixes and completion observability, (4) all six edits
with same-cell, choice/merge, and gate-borrowing cases, (5) receipt-silent
alias progress, and (6) both fresh--install and alias--install orders.
If progress occurs first, it invalidates the seal, whereas installation first
closes the fixture's old program generation.  All 19 focused and 81 repository
tests pass, including the focused suite after clean archive extraction.

\paragraph{Falsifiability}
The executable subset fails if resolution loses an occurrence, a fixed-point
step lacks its witness, \(W^\dagger\) differs from the survivor-prefix
language, or a stale cut succeeds.  The theorem fails if refinement drops an
obligation, the monitor language differs, an old epoch remains enabled, or
the LTSs differ observably.  The separate Lean endpoint model does not prove
\cref{thm:exact-history}.  Stronger mechanization remains future work.
