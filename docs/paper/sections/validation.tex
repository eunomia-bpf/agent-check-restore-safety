\section{Mechanization and Executable Validation}
% 机械化与可执行验证
\label{sec:validation}

Five Lean modules pass \texttt{--trust=0} and machine-check the finite fixed-point correspondence and the edit, installation, and race theorems encoded by the artifact.
% 五个 Lean 模块通过 \texttt{--trust=0} 检查，并机器检验制品中编码的有限不动点对应关系以及编辑、安装与竞态定理。

All 19 paper-specific tests pass, together with 62 authority and compiler regression tests for supporting components.
% 全部 19 项论文专用测试以及面向支撑组件的 62 项权限与编译器回归测试均通过。
Across accepted instances with 2--128 outcomes, checker time is \(0.11\)--\(53.47\) ms; rejected instances take \(0.11\)--\(5.51\) ms, and enumerating 6--720 unordered completions takes \(0.33\)--\(50.35\) ms.
% 在具有 2--128 个结果的接受实例中，检查器耗时为 \(0.11\)--\(53.47\) ms；拒绝实例耗时为 \(0.11\)--\(5.51\) ms，枚举 6--720 条无序完成路径耗时为 \(0.33\)--\(50.35\) ms。
