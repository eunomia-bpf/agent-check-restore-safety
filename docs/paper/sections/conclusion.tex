\section{Conclusion}
% 结论
\label{sec:conclusion}

Safe execution editing has an exact criterion: past protected actions remain immutable, and every still-required outcome retains a policy-safe completion.
% 安全执行编辑具有一个精确判据：过去的受控动作保持不可变，并且每个仍要求实现的结果都保留一条策略安全的完成路径。
Our checker turns every registered Fork, Restore, Merge, or extension into the unique most-permissive monitor or a finite rejection certificate.
% 我们的检查器把每项已注册的 Fork、Restore、Merge 或扩展转化为唯一且限制最少的监控器，或一份有限拒绝证书。
The Exact Edit-Safety theorem unifies exact synthesis, the four necessary authenticated-state relations, atomic race serialization, repeated finite execution, and ideal--runtime equivalence, providing a formal foundation for branchable Agent runtimes.
% 精确编辑安全定理统一了精确综合、四类必要认证状态关系、原子竞态串行化、重复有限执行以及理想机—运行时等价，为可分支 Agent 运行时提供形式化基础。
