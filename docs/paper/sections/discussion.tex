\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{Scope.}
Snapshots cannot retract external effects or receipts, and total rollback is
wrong when histories remain live.  The problem extends to workflows,
deployments, notebooks, search, and RL; agents make it routine by constructing
the graph online around external tools.  Edge-local certificates compose until
receipt growth forces readmission.  Hermetic choose-one rollouts may delay
authority; parallel or merged external-state rollouts cannot.

\paragraph{Assumptions and nonclaims.}
Atomic redemption protects a cell; joint control protects correlations.
$\mathsf{RawPhysical}$ composes declared co-live controllers, so hidden
coordination may reject safely while an unmodeled bypass is unsound.  Lean
proves a finite manifest theorem, not a runtime; Python and its parser are
unverified refinements.  Truthful evidence, complete mediation, freshness, and
redemption remain runtime obligations.  The seal never authorizes effects or
proves exactly-once sinks, liveness, reward correctness, or safe learning.

\section{Conclusion}

History admission derives the correlated future of Fork, Restore, or Merge,
conditions it on the durable past, normalizes cells separately from
controllers, and certifies or refutes its realization.  It admits an edge only
if its required future survives and its realizable future cannot exceed the
admitted one.
