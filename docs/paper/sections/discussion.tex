\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{Scope}
Snapshots cannot retract external effects or receipts, and total rollback is
wrong when histories remain live.  The model covers workflows, deployments,
notebooks, search, and RL; agents make the graph online around external tools.
Edge certificates compose until receipt growth forces readmission.  Hermetic
choose-one rollouts can defer authority; parallel external commits and durable
joins require admission at their history edge.

\paragraph{Assumptions and nonclaims}
Atomic redemption protects a cell; joint control protects correlations.
$\mathsf{RawPhysical}$ composes declared controllers: hidden coordination can
reject safely, whereas an unmodeled bypass is unsound.  $\Gamma$ binds one
co-durable epoch; receipt or controller-version change forces readmission.
Lean proves the finite manifest theorem, while Python recomputes full $\Gamma$
under attested coverage.  Its repair is greatest only among downward-closed
restrictions of submitted $\Gamma$ at fixed $E,L$; it must be installed and
resubmitted, and says nothing about $\mathsf{Actual}$.  Truthful evidence,
mediation, freshness, and redemption remain runtime obligations.  The
contract-only arity is worst-case tight, though concrete structure may lower
it.  The seal authorizes no effect and proves neither exactly-once execution
nor safe learning.

\section{Conclusion}

History admission derives the correlated future of Fork, Restore, or Merge,
conditions it on the durable past, normalizes redemption cells separately from
controllers, prunes submitted co-liveness, and derives a sufficient observation
arity with an arbitrary-$k$ tightness family.  It seals only when
$\mathsf{Required}\subseteq\mathsf{RawPhysical}
\subseteq\mathsf{Admitted}$; sound runtime use additionally requires
$\mathsf{Required}\subseteq\mathsf{Actual}
\subseteq\mathsf{RawPhysical}$.
