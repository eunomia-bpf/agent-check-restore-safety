\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{Scope}
Snapshots cannot retract effects or receipts, and total rollback is wrong while
histories remain live.  Each edge decision fixes one receipt frontier; growth
forces readmission.  Choose-one rollouts may defer authority, but parallel
commits and durable joins require edge admission.

\paragraph{Assumptions and nonclaims}
Atomic redemption protects cells; joint control protects correlations.
$\mathsf{RawPhysical}$ composes declared controllers: hidden coordination may
reject, whereas an unmodeled bypass is unsound.  $\Gamma$ binds one epoch;
receipt or controller-version changes force readmission.
Projection readiness assumes exact projection, hidden downward closure, and
fixed $E,L$; sound upper bounds never seal.  Repair is greatest only relative
to submitted evidence and must be installed and resubmitted.  Truthful evidence,
runtime refinement, mediation, freshness, and redemption remain external
obligations.
The seal authorizes no effect and proves neither exactly-once nor safe learning.

\paragraph{Expressiveness}
The model covers finite, unordered, downward-closed safety contracts over
unit-normalized prospective commitments.  Temporal order, causal enablement,
nonmonotone obligations, and an atomic multi-effect bundle are not represented
directly; an adapter must encode the relevant unit as a cell or use a richer
semantics.  Unknown future calls are covered only when they redeem a
predeclared authority slot.  A new slot, controller version, or contract
requires readmission rather than optimistic extension of an old seal.

\section{Conclusion}

History admission gives Fork, Restore, and Merge a receipt-conditioned future
and a sufficient structural view with explicit worst-case lower bounds.  Its
seal transports only if
$\mathsf{Required}\subseteq\mathsf{Actual}
\subseteq\mathsf{Raw}(E,L,\Gamma)$; readiness supplies
$\mathsf{Raw}(E,L,\Gamma)\subseteq\mathsf{Admitted}$.
