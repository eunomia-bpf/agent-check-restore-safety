\section{Threshold-Guarded Contracts}
\label{sec:algorithm}

The family \(\Phi\) is semantic; a runtime still needs a compact, durable representation.
Choice/parallel syntax is efficient but is not closed under exact promotion repair (\cref{prop:nonclosure}).

\paragraph{Base contracts.}
The conflict graph induced by a unique-leaf structured contract is a cograph: \(\Box\) is graph join and \(\parallel\) is disjoint union.
The classical cotree recurrence is
\begin{align*}
R(\mathbf0)&=0,& R(b)&=r_b,\\
R(P\Box Q)&=R(P)\vee R(Q),&
R(P\parallel Q)&=R(P)+R(Q).
\end{align*}
Thus \(R(P)=\bigvee_{C\in\front(P)}q(C)\) in \(O(|P||\calK|)\) time~\cite{corneil1985cographs}.

\paragraph{Capability factorization.}
For an unguarded cotree, one bottom-up/top-down pass computes every leaf's maximum co-load and hence every \(H(b)\).
Then \cref{eq:rectangularity} is equivalent to
\[
\forall C\in\front(P).\quad
\sum_{b\in C}(r_b+H(b))\le G-d,
\]
which is another max/sum cotree evaluation with adjusted leaf weights.
Thus, in the fixed-topology batch-Reserve fragment, the monitor decides in \(O(|P||\calK|)\) whether the exact admission set is a Cartesian product of branch-local budgets.
For a guarded contract, the same condition is checked by the global oracle and can return a violating configuration as a certificate.

\paragraph{Frozen threshold guards.}
A guarded contract \(T=(P,\mathcal H)\) intersects \(\front(P)\) with nonnegative vector inequalities.
A guard has residual \(r_h\) and frozen lineage terms \((a_i,\lambda_i)\):
\[
C\models h
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\sum_i a_i\lambda_i(C)\le r_h,
\]
where \(a_i\in\Nat^{\calK}\) and each explicitly represented monotone, zero-preserving predicate \(\lambda_i(C)\in\{0,1\}\) records whether a stable branch lineage is represented in \(C\).
Nonnegativity preserves downward closure.
Initially, \(\lambda_b(C)=\mathbf1[b\in C]\).

The promotion row \(\gamma_U\) in \cref{eq:promotion-guard} is one such guard.
By \cref{thm:guard-closure}, at most \(|\calK|\) scalar inequalities, \(O(|B||\calK|)\) coefficients, and singleton lineage tests represent the unique largest topology-only safe restriction for fixed target claims.
Constructing this row takes \(O(|B||\calK|)\) space and time; checking owner support and global safety under accumulated guards may still require the guarded oracle.

The row is \emph{frozen} when installed under a no-silent-policy-expansion rule.
If withdrawing the claim of \(x\) recomputed its coefficient as zero, the triple \(\{x,y,z\}\) would silently reappear.
The dynamically relaxed family may be authority-safe; nevertheless, relaxing a durable audited restriction requires an explicit topology-expansion admission.
Reserve likewise changes the objective checked against the contract; it does not mutate old guards.

\paragraph{Lineage transport.}
Lifecycle refinement composes old lineage predicates with the operation's explicit projection.
For live restore of \(b\) as \(b\parallel b'\),
\[
z_b\ \leftarrow\ z_b\lor z_{b'}.
\]
Thus retaining both continuations pays an old guard coefficient once, rather than twice as coefficient copying would.
The specified replace and fork rules use rename and descendant OR respectively; a general merge must supply a monotone, zero-preserving projection satisfying \cref{eq:simulation}, or use full target admission.
The guards and substitutions reside in the non-rollbackable controller state.

\paragraph{Admission boundary.}
Given guards, global admission computes, for every coordinate \(k\),
\[
\max_{C\in\Phi(T)}\sum_{b\in C}\sum_{c\in Q_b}w_k(c).
\]
This boundary is inherently harder than evaluating one selected frontier.
With all branches parallel and one scalar guard \(\sum_i a_i z_i\le r\), asking whether an allowed frontier has value above \(G\) is \textsc{Knapsack}; unsafety is NP-complete and universal safety is coNP-complete.
A concrete frontier and its guard membership remain linearly checkable in the stored coefficient-plus-predicate-circuit representation.
The unguarded cotree is the fast path; guarded implementations can use an incremental pseudo-Boolean solver or a decision diagram, without changing the semantics.
Prepare can construct \(\gamma_U\) in linear time before dispatch, although support certificates may require the oracle.
Reserve and general merge require checked admission unless a structural simulation applies.
Nonduplicating fork and restore with OR transport are fast paths only when all simulation premises hold.

\paragraph{Serial or seal.}
Boundary~II makes final-owner support a checkable scheduler condition.
If it holds, any owner-group order is safe under exact repair and cleanup.
If it fails, at least one order is disabled; the controller must validate a particular order, coordinate cleanup, or atomically install the fixed batch before dispatch.
