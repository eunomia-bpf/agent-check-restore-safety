\section{Compilation and Durable Refinement}
\label{sec:kernel}

\subsection{Occurrence-sensitive compilation}

Compilation maps the greatest realization to a canonical installable program.
Finite pomsets make \(W=W^\dagger_{H,u}\) and \(B=B^\dagger_{H,u}\) finite.
For \(L/z=\{v\mid zv\in L\}\), use residual states
\(q_z=(W^\dagger/z,B^\dagger/z)\).
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:canonical-program}
\begin{aligned}
 Q_{W,B} &= \{q_z\mid z\in W\},\\
 q^0&=q_\epsilon=(W^\dagger,B^\dagger),\\
 q_z\xrightarrow{a}q_{za}
 &\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad za\in W .
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
Write \(\mathsf{Can}(W,B)\) for this automaton.  Every state is reachable and
marked exactly when \(\epsilon\in B^\dagger/z\).  Transition symbol \(a\) contains
occurrence \(x\), cell \(d\), and \Fresh/\Alias mode, preventing use through
another occurrence's gate.

\begin{lemma}[Exact compiled realization]
\label{lem:compiled-language}
The generated and marked languages of the program in
\cref{eq:canonical-program} are exactly
\((W^\dagger_{H,u},B^\dagger_{H,u})\).  The program is the smallest deterministic marked
partial automaton for this pair up to state renaming.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Induction on \(z\) reaches \((W^\dagger/z,B^\dagger/z)\), enables \(a\) iff
\(za\in W^\dagger\), and marks the state iff \(z\in B^\dagger\).  Distinct
states differ on a generated or marked suffix and must remain distinct.  This
is the paired-language derivative construction
~\cite{brzozowski1964derivatives}.
\end{proof}

\(\mathsf{Compile}(\kappa,H,\Delta,\mathcal A,u)\) runs the schema judgment.
Failure returns the failed structural check.  Otherwise it computes
\(\widehat B^\dagger_{H,u}\) using \cref{eq:robust-operator} and applies the
admission test in \cref{eq:admission}.  An empty fixed point returns its chain
and ranked causes.
Success emits \(\widehat B^\dagger\), the complete chain and coverage
witnesses,
\(\mathsf{prog}_Z=\mathsf{Can}(W^\dagger,B^\dagger)\), and a seal
\[
 Z=(\omega,\zeta,\kappa_Z,\mathsf{cut}_Z,u,
    H_{\mathsf{prog}},\eta^-,\eta^+),
\]
where \(\kappa_Z=\kappa\), \(H_c=H\) is the authenticated pre-installation
history, and the history judgment mints \(\eta^+\).

Let \(\mathsf{enc}\) be canonical and length-delimited, with \(\kappa\)
authenticating \(\mathcal A\).  The symbolic LTS uses domain-separated
injective authenticated constructors \(\mathsf{Seal}_{\rm prog}\) and
\(\mathsf{Seal}_{\rm cut}\), so equal seals have equal payloads.  Define
\[
 H_{\mathsf{prog}}=
 \mathsf{Seal}_{\rm prog}(
   \mathsf{enc}(\mathsf{prog}_Z,\widehat B^\dagger_{H,u})).
\]
The cut seal is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:cut-seal}
 \mathsf{cut}_Z=
 \mathsf{Seal}_{\rm cut}(\mathsf{enc}(\omega,\zeta,\kappa,H,\Delta,u,
     \mathcal C_{H,u},H_{\mathsf{prog}},\eta^-,\eta^+)).
\end{equation}
Because \(H\) contains \(\chi,\nu,\rho,\beta\), the cut binds logical progress,
the history view, registry state, and gate/epoch bindings.  Search and inactive preloading are untrusted.  The installation
checker re-derives the schema judgment and complete indexed fixed point,
reconstructs
\((W^\dagger,B^\dagger)\), and verifies
\[
 \mathsf{prog}_Z\cong\mathsf{Can}(W^\dagger,B^\dagger),
\]
preserving the initial state, marking, and each labeled transition.  It verifies
both seals and the full automaton isomorphism above.  A byte implementation may use
signatures or collision-resistant digests over \(\mathsf{enc}\), making
refinement computational up to forgery or collision.  Exact bisimulation uses
the symbolic constructors.

\subsection{Durable kernel}

The compiled state for one policy domain is
\[
 S=(\kappa,H,\Delta,\mathsf{out},
    \mathsf{phase},\mathsf{bind},\mathsf{prog},\mathsf q,\mathsf{cert}),
\]
where every epoch has phase
\(\mathsf{inactive}\), \(\mathsf{active}\), or \(\mathsf{closed}\).
\(\mathsf{cert}(\eta)\) is the checked installation record
\((H_c,u,W^\dagger,\widehat B^\dagger,Z)\) for an active epoch.  Its indexed
family is proof-only ghost state, while \(W^\dagger\), \(B^\dagger\), and the seal
are checked enforcement data.
The following invariant clauses are required:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=1pt]
\item exactly the current epoch \(\eta\) is active and every live occurrence
       has one authenticated handle for its gate, which is bound to \(\eta\);
 \item inactive preloaded epochs own no live gate or available handle; arbitrary unreachable
       inactive programs may be discarded as preload garbage;
 \item the current certificate agrees with the admitted cut and projects to
       the active program's generated/marked residual after the current prefix;
 \item every resolved occurrence appears once in ordered \(\chi\);
 \item every \Fresh in \(\chi\) has exactly one receipt in \(\Delta\), and
       every \Alias names an earlier receipt;
 \item a closed epoch never becomes active again.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Atomic protected use}
For occurrence \(x\) bound to gate \(j\) in epoch \(\eta\), handle \(h\)
authenticates cell \(d\).
\(\mathsf{VerifyHandle}(h,\rho(x),j,d)\) checks the protected-call issuer,
scope, digest, gate, and cell.  Resolve \(a\) as \(\Fresh(x,d)\) if \(d\notin D_\Delta\) and
\(\Alias(x,d)\) otherwise.  Write \(\Use_x=\mathsf{Use}(x,j,h)\).
Its one durable transition is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:use-rule}
\frac{
\begin{gathered}
 x\text{ enabled in }T\quad
 \mathsf{bind}(x)=(j,\eta)\\[-2pt]
 \eta=H.\mathsf{epoch}\quad
 \mathsf{phase}(\eta)=\mathsf{active}\\[-2pt]
 \mathsf{VerifyHandle}(h,\rho(x),j,d)\quad
 \mathsf q(\eta)\xrightarrow{a}_{\mathsf{prog}(\eta)}q'
\end{gathered}}
{S\xrightarrow{\Use_x/a}S'} .
\end{equation}
Let \(T/(x,a)\) be the unique structured residual that removes \(x\) from its leaf
contract and appends \(a\) to that leaf's resolved cursor.  If \(x\) lies below
an open choice, \(T/(x,a)\) also selects the unique arm containing \(x\).  All
frontier constructors remain present.  Then
\(\mathsf{Advance}(S,x,a,q')\) changes exactly
\[
\begin{aligned}
 H.T&:=T/(x,a),& H.\chi&:=\chi a,& H.\nu&:=\nu+1,\\
 H.\beta&:=\beta\setminus\{x\},&
 \mathsf{bind}&:=\mathsf{bind}\setminus\{x\},&
 \mathsf q(\eta)&:=q'.
\end{aligned}
\]
For \Fresh it additionally appends the immutable labeled receipt and outbox
item.  For \Alias it records the link to the cell's durable result future
without changing \(\Delta\).  Every other field is unchanged.

A request naming an already-resolved \(x\) is treated as a \Retry only when
\(\mathsf{VerifyRetry}(h,x,j,d,\chi,\Delta)\) authenticates the retry handle
through the \(\chi\)-to-receipt link.  On success, the kernel returns the
stable result without a transition.  The kernel applies \cref{eq:use-rule} to
a request naming an unresolved \(x\) iff all premises hold.  Any failure,
including a missing transition, bad handle, unbound
occurrence, disabled branch, or closed epoch, returns \(\mathsf{deny}(x)\)
without changing state.  \Dispatch releases only the oldest outbox item.
Settlement and crash preserve security state.

\paragraph{Atomic installation}
At commit time the kernel ignores caller-supplied target or epoch data.  It
re-runs
\[
\Sigma_\zeta;H\vdash u\Downarrow
 (H',\mathcal C,\eta^-,\eta^+)
\]
over the current history.  \(\mathsf{ReCut}(S,u,Z)\) is undefined if this
judgment has no derivation and otherwise equals the right-hand side of
\cref{eq:cut-seal} recomputed from current state.
\(\mathsf{Verify}_Z(W,B,\widehat B)\) holds exactly when the trusted
installation checker recomputes \(\widehat B_0\) and every fixed-point
iteration, verifies every ranked removal cause and surviving coverage witness,
requires the stabilized \(\widehat B\ne\varnothing\), reconstructs
\(B=\pi_2\widehat B\) and \(W=\Pref(B)\), checks
\(\mathsf{prog}_Z\cong\mathsf{Can}(W,B)\), and verifies the program seal.
Installation requires
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:install-premise}
\begin{gathered}
 \kappa=\kappa_Z,\qquad
 \mathsf{ReCut}(S,u,Z)=\mathsf{cut}_Z,\\
 \mathsf{Verify}_Z(W^\dagger_{H,u},B^\dagger_{H,u},
    \widehat B^\dagger_{H,u}),\\
 H.\mathsf{epoch}=\eta^-,\qquad
 \mathsf{phase}(\eta^+)=\mathsf{inactive}.
\end{gathered}
\end{equation}
If all checks hold, one domain transaction verifies that every target binding
in \(H'\) names a new gate owned by \(\eta^+\), and that every
\((x,j_x^+,h_x^+)\in\mathcal H_u^+\) passes
\(\mathsf{VerifyHandle}(h_x^+,\rho'(x),j_x^+,q_{\rm cell}^{\rho'}(x))\).
It then applies
\[
\begin{aligned}
 H&:=H',&
 \mathsf{phase}(\eta^-)&:=\mathsf{closed},\\
 \mathsf{phase}(\eta^+)&:=\mathsf{active},&
 \mathsf{bind}|_{X_{\sem{T'}}}&:=\beta',\\
 \mathsf{prog}(\eta^+)&:=\mathsf{prog}_Z,&
 \mathsf q(\eta^+)&:=q^0,\\
 \mathsf{cert}(\eta^+)&:=
   (H_c,u,W^\dagger,\widehat B^\dagger,Z).
\end{aligned}
\]
All unrelated inactive/closed records are preserved.  The transaction makes
the stable bundle \(\mathcal H_u^+\) available only after \(\eta^+\) is active.
A lost reply is retrieved from the durable target registry after recovery.
Old handles for unresolved occurrences fail the refreshed registry/gate check,
whereas a same-invocation \Retry follows its archived
\(\chi\)-to-receipt link.  The successful step is observed as
\(\mathsf{Edge}(u)\), whose notation suppresses the schema-determined bundle.
Failure is an internal stutter with no state change.

\paragraph{Remaining rules}
\(\mathsf{Preload}(\eta,Z)\) is a \(\tau\)-step that may change only an
inactive, unbound epoch.  Failed installation is a state-preserving
\(\tau\)-step.  \(\mathsf{Save}\) takes the trusted checkpoint transition
defined in \cref{sec:model}.  Authenticated retry, bundle retrieval, and result
delivery are \(\tau\)-steps that return their durable value without changing
the security core.
Settlement monotonically fills the corresponding durable result future, and
crash is a security-state
self-loop.  \(\Dispatch(d)\) is enabled only for the outbox head and marks
exactly that entry released.  These clauses, together with the total denial
rule above, make the compiled LTS transition relation explicit.

\begin{lemma}[History-cut linearization]
\label{lem:cut-linearization}
In every reachable well-formed compiled state, a successful \Install and a
protected use in the same policy domain have a unique order.  If
the use commits first, that seal cannot install.  If \Install commits first,
the old use cannot resolve \Fresh or \Alias.  Steps in independent domains
commute by product semantics.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Use and installation serialize on the same history and epoch records.
\Fresh changes \((\Delta,\chi,\nu)\), while \Alias changes \((\chi,\nu)\).
Because \(\mathsf{cut}_Z\) authenticates \(H\) and \(\Delta\), a prior use
invalidates the seal.  Installation first closes the old epoch, so
\cref{eq:use-rule} rejects every old binding.  Disjoint cells, receipt ledgers, and
policies give the product case.  Persisting \(H'\), target bindings, \(q^0\),
and the checked certificate restores the invariant.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Decidability and trust}

The installation checker enumerates indexed linearizations or their asynchronous product
with the policy automaton, then performs at most
\(|\widehat B_0|\) strict-removal rounds.  Parallel pomsets make the worst case
exponential: decidability is finite for bounded slices, not polynomial.  The
TCB comprises registries, receipt ledger and atomic rules, the policy issuer
and protected-call issuer, certificate verification, and complete mediation.
Search, minimization, and preloading remain untrusted because
\(\mathsf{Verify}_Z\) and \Install independently re-establish the required
invariants.
