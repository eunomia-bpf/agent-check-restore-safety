\section{The Agent History-Admission Theorem}
\label{sec:results}

This section gives one characterization of when the reference monitor for an
Agent history edge can be compiled into a post-edge controller epoch.  The
older family, obstruction, refinement, and evidence results appear as lemmas
supporting that characterization rather than as independent contribution
claims.

Fix a valid typed edge $e$, authority family $\mathcal A$, durable frontier
$D\in\mathcal A$, and lineage $\ell$.  Write
$\mathsf{Cand}_e$, $\mathsf{Req}_e$, and $\mathsf{Adm}_e(D)$ for its candidate,
required, and admitted endpoint families.  For a distinct-cell trace $\sigma$,
let $\operatorname{cells}(\sigma)$ be its endpoint set.  The operational global
monitor $\mathsf{GM}_e(D)$ starts at $\varnothing$ and admits a fresh cell $d$
exactly when the extended endpoint belongs to $\mathsf{Adm}_e(D)$.  For a
normalized controller manifest, define
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathsf{Phys}(E,L,\Gamma)=
 \{\sigma\mid{}&\sigma\text{ contains distinct cells and}\\[-2pt]
 &\operatorname{cells}(\sigma)\in
   \mathsf{RawPhysical}(E,L,\Gamma)\}.
\end{aligned}
\]
Thus $\mathsf{GM}_e(D)$ is the serialized reference semantics, whereas
$\mathsf{Phys}$ is the language of controllers allowed to commit without a
call to that monitor at every effect.

\subsection{Family lemmas used by the characterization}

\begin{lemma}[Typed generation]
\label{thm:typed-generation}
For every valid instance of the six edge relations in
\cref{sec:semantics}, $\mathsf{Cand}_e$ and $\mathsf{Req}_e$ are nonempty,
contain $\varnothing$, and are downward closed, with
\[
  \mathsf{Req}_e\subseteq\mathsf{Cand}_e.
\]
\end{lemma}

The proof is structural.  Choice is family union; parallel or live composition
is tensor; replacement selects the checkpoint contract.  The result checks
composition of supplied leaf contracts, not their truth about a runtime.

For compactness, write
\[
\begin{aligned}
  \mathsf{PCM}(\mathcal A,D,\mathcal F,\ell)
  \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad{}&
  D\in\mathcal A\\[-2pt]
  &{}\land\forall X\in\mathcal F.\ \mathsf{PrefixOK}_D(X).
\end{aligned}
\]

\begin{lemma}[Greatest fixed-frontier monitor family]
\label{thm:greatest-residual}
The family $\mathsf{Adm}_e(D)$ in \cref{eq:admitted-future} satisfies
\[
\mathsf{PCM}(\mathcal A,D,\mathsf{Adm}_e(D),\ell)
\quad\text{and}\quad
\mathsf{Adm}_e(D)\subseteq\mathsf{Cand}_e.
\]
For every $\mathcal H\subseteq\mathsf{Cand}_e$,
\[
  \mathsf{PCM}(\mathcal A,D,\mathcal H,\ell)
  \Longrightarrow \mathcal H\subseteq\mathsf{Adm}_e(D).
\]
Consequently,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:required-preservable-result}
\begin{aligned}
\mathsf{Req}_e\subseteq\mathsf{Adm}_e(D)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\exists\mathcal H.\quad&
\mathsf{Req}_e\subseteq\mathcal H
\subseteq\mathsf{Cand}_e\\[-2pt]
&{}\land\mathsf{PCM}(\mathcal A,D,\mathcal H,\ell).
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
\end{lemma}

This is a greatest-language fact only inside the fixed candidate, authority
family, frontier, and lineage.  It is classical pruning machinery specialized
to the family derived by the Agent edge.

\subsection{Exact monitor compilation}

History admissibility asks for two properties simultaneously.  It is
\emph{monitor-sound} when
$\mathsf{Phys}(E,L,\Gamma)\subseteq
\mathsf{Traces}(\mathsf{GM}_e(D))$.  It is
\emph{required-transparent} when every $X\in\mathsf{Req}_e$ is the endpoint of
some trace in $\mathsf{Phys}(E,L,\Gamma)$.  The second condition matters even
when a global monitor would eventually reject every unsafe commit: an Agent
edge that promises a join but is certain to fail only at its final required
effect is not a transparent compilation.

Let
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:required-coliveness-arity}
\begin{aligned}
r_e=\max\{&
 \max_{X\in\mathsf{Req}_e}|X|,
 \max_{K\in\mathsf{MNF}(\mathsf{Adm}_e(D))}|K|,\\[-2pt]
&\mathbf 1[
 \operatorname{supp}(\mathsf{Adm}_e(D))\ne\mathcal D]\},
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
where an empty maximum is zero.  The final term retains unary
outside-support evidence.

\begin{theorem}[Agent History-Admission / Monitor-Compilation]
\label{thm:agent-history-admission}
Assume exact normalized cell and controller identities, a truthful complete
manifest, and downward-closed controller-local and co-liveness families.
Then:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt]
  \item the reference monitor has exactly the admitted language,
  \begin{equation}
  \label{eq:global-monitor-language}
  \begin{aligned}
  \mathsf{Traces}(\mathsf{GM}_e(D))
  =\{\sigma\mid{}&
    \sigma\text{ is distinct-cell},\\[-2pt]
    &\operatorname{cells}(\sigma)\in\mathsf{Adm}_e(D)\}.
  \end{aligned}
  \end{equation}

  \item monitor soundness and required transparency have the exact endpoint
  characterization
  \begin{equation}
  \label{eq:history-admission-equivalence}
  \boxed{
  \begin{aligned}
  &\mathsf{HistoryAdmissible}_D(e;E,L,\Gamma)\\
  &\quad\Longleftrightarrow\
   \mathsf{DeploymentReady}_D(e;E,L,\Gamma)\\
  &\quad\Longleftrightarrow\
   \mathsf{Req}_e
   \subseteq\mathsf{RawPhysical}(E,L,\Gamma)
   \subseteq\mathsf{Adm}_e(D)\\
  &\quad\Longleftrightarrow\
  \mathsf{HA}\!\left(
    e,\mathcal A,D,q_{\rm cell},\ell,q_{\rm ctrl},\right.\\[-2pt]
  &\hspace{24mm}\left.
    E,L,\Pi_{\le r_e}(\Gamma)
  \right)=\mathsf{accept};
  \end{aligned}}
  \end{equation}
  here $\mathsf{HA}$ is the finite structural checker and the last equivalence
  assumes an attested exact $r_e$-projection of the same installed $\Gamma$;
  and

  \item if a mediated runtime establishes
  $\mathsf{Req}_e\subseteq\mathsf{Actual}$ and
  $\mathsf{ActualTrace}\subseteq\mathsf{Phys}(E,L,\Gamma)$,
  an accepted seal transports both conclusions to the runtime:
  every actual trace belongs to
  $\mathsf{Traces}(\mathsf{GM}_e(D))$ and every required endpoint remains
  realizable.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
For \cref{eq:global-monitor-language}, the monitor guard maintains membership
in $\mathsf{Adm}_e(D)$ after every step.  Conversely, take any
$X\in\mathsf{Adm}_e(D)$.  Downward closure places every prefix set of every
enumeration of $X$ in the same family, so the monitor accepts that enumeration.
This proves language equality, not merely monitor soundness.

The endpoints of $\mathsf{Phys}(E,L,\Gamma)$ are exactly
$\mathsf{RawPhysical}(E,L,\Gamma)$ by the cover semantics.  Substituting the
reference-language equality into the two definitions of history admissibility
therefore yields the sandwich in
\cref{eq:history-admission-equivalence}.  The contract bound $r_e$ preserves
both required coverage and every safety obstruction at fixed $E,L$, giving
the checker equivalence.  The runtime clause is inclusion composition.
\end{proof}

The global monitor is intentionally the strongest baseline: under complete
serialized mediation it prevents replay and forbidden unions without knowing
the future Agent graph.  The theorem does not say history admission is
necessary for such a monitor to be safe.  It says exactly when that monitor can
be replaced for one edge by the submitted controller epoch without either
expanding its language or silently losing required behavior.

\subsection{Necessity and the coordination consequence}

The sandwich yields a sharp three-way boundary.  If
$\mathsf{Req}_e\nsubseteq\mathsf{Adm}_e(D)$, then even the global monitor
cannot preserve the fixed edge contract; by
\cref{eq:required-preservable-result}, no pruning-only compilation can repair
it.  If the required family survives but the physical language escapes
$\mathsf{Adm}_e(D)$, the detached epoch must restrict joint commitment,
coordinate through a shared monitor or reservation point, or be rejected.  If
the physical language is safe but misses $\mathsf{Req}_e$, accepting it would
change the meaning of the typed edge.

These alternatives can be decided constructively.  For
$\mathcal R=\mathsf{Req}_e$, $\mathcal F=\mathsf{Adm}_e(D)$, and
$\mathcal P=\mathsf{RawPhysical}(E,L,\Gamma)$, let
$\mathsf{MNF}(\mathcal F)$ be the minimal nonfaces of $\mathcal F$.

\begin{lemma}[Complete physical obstructions]
\label{thm:deployment-obstructions}
If every $L_g$ is downward closed, then
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal R\subseteq\mathcal P\subseteq\mathcal F
\Longleftrightarrow{}&
\mathcal R\subseteq\mathcal P\\[-2pt]
&{}\land\bigl(\forall C\in\mathcal P.\
  C\subseteq\operatorname{supp}(\mathcal F)\bigr)\\[-2pt]
&{}\land\bigl(\forall K\in\mathsf{MNF}(\mathcal F).\
  K\notin\mathcal P\bigr).
\end{aligned}
\]
Without local downward closure, failure still has the exact decomposition
\[
\neg(\mathcal R\subseteq\mathcal P\subseteq\mathcal F)
\Longleftrightarrow
(\mathcal P\setminus\mathcal F\ne\varnothing)
\lor(\mathcal R\setminus\mathcal P\ne\varnothing).
\]
The two differences may both be nonempty.
\end{lemma}

The support notation in the first display abbreviates that every
$C\in\mathcal P$ is contained in $\operatorname{supp}(\mathcal F)$.  Minimal
nonfaces are complete only after excluding this support escape.  They are
classical configuration-structure obstructions
~\cite{vanglabbeek2009configuration,winskel1987event}, not a new combinatorial
object introduced here.

For fixed $E,L$, write
$\mathsf{Raw}(\Delta)=\mathsf{RawPhysical}(E,L,\Delta)$ and define
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:principal-coliveness-repair}
\Gamma^\star_{\mathcal F}
=\{G\in\Gamma\mid \mathsf{Raw}(2^G)\subseteq\mathcal F\}.
\end{equation}

\begin{lemma}[Greatest co-liveness compilation]
\label{prop:principal-coliveness-repair}
If $\Gamma$ is downward closed, then
$\Gamma^\star_{\mathcal F}$ is its greatest downward-closed restriction whose
physical language is contained in $\mathcal F$.  Moreover,
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\mathcal R\subseteq\mathsf{Raw}(\Gamma^\star_{\mathcal F})\\
&\quad\Longleftrightarrow
\exists\Delta\subseteq\Gamma.\quad
\Delta\text{ downward closed}\\[-2pt]
&\hspace{31mm}{}\land\
\mathcal R\subseteq\mathsf{Raw}(\Delta)\subseteq\mathcal F.
\end{aligned}
\]
\end{lemma}

Thus failure on $\Gamma^\star_{\mathcal F}$ is a necessity result within the
declared repair space: no restriction of controller co-liveness alone can
compile the monitor transparently at fixed $E,L$.  A runtime must instead add
a shared serialization/reservation mechanism, change the controller
realization, change the edge contract under new authorization, or reject the
edge.  Merely dropping a required outcome is not history admission.  This is a
specialized range-control consequence, not a claim that all coordination
problems reduce to our model.

For diagnostics, a fixed bad cover is classified as support escape, local
overpermission, or a correlation cut in which every local piece is admitted
but their union is not.  The cases are exclusive for that cover, not globally
unique across all covers.  Under local downward closure and local soundness, a
support-contained overpermission contains a physically realizable minimal
nonface; a genuine correlation cut has at least two active controllers.
These extraction lemmas explain a failed compilation but do not add a second
security property.

\subsection{Composition across Agent edges and receipt growth}

\begin{lemma}[Edge and frontier evolution]
\label{thm:structural-transport}
At a fixed durable frontier $D$, a version-monotone structural edge refinement
that is injective on each target configuration, maps every target
configuration into the old admitted family, and commutes with lineage
transports the old prefix certificate.  Such refinements compose, including
version order, configuration injectivity, and the lineage square.

A seal is bound to $D$.  When a fresh \Prepare{} grows the ledger frontier to
$D'\supsetneq D$, that seal cannot authorize a subsequent edge: admission must
be recomputed against $D'$.  If the candidate and lineage are unchanged, then
$\mathsf{Adm}_e(D')\subseteq\mathsf{Adm}_e(D)$.
\end{lemma}

The fixed-frontier composition supports an execution graph discovered online;
it does not require the complete Agent plan in advance.  The frontier rule is
security-relevant rather than a cache invalidation convention: a restored cell
whose lineage atom enters $D'$ is removed even when a snapshot parent map still
exists.

Operationally, a version-bound installation must linearize the proof step.  It
checks that the edge source version, $D$, contracts, identities, and manifest
still match the seal; closes the old epoch; and only then publishes the new
controllers.  A concurrent \Prepare{} or version change makes the comparison
fail.  Conditional on this atomic edge cut, the old and new epochs cannot both
prepare effects across the proof boundary.  This installation bridge is not
implemented by the offline compiler and is not part of the Lean development.

\begin{lemma}[Runtime certificate transport]
\label{thm:runtime-transport}
If the manifest satisfies the history-admission sandwich and the runtime
establishes
$\mathsf{Req}_e\subseteq\mathsf{Actual}
\subseteq\mathsf{RawPhysical}$, then
\[
\mathsf{Req}_e\subseteq\mathsf{Actual}\subseteq\mathsf{Adm}_e(D)
\quad\text{and}\quad
\mathsf{PCM}(\mathcal A,D,\mathsf{Actual},\ell).
\]
\end{lemma}

This conclusion is conditional on truthful manifests, complete mediation, and
the two inclusions involving $\mathsf{Actual}$; the compiler proves none of
those premises.

\subsection{Tight evidence for an exact verdict}

The exact checker does not require a full listing of arbitrarily large
controller sets.  At fixed $E,L$, the contract determines how much co-liveness
evidence is sufficient.

\begin{lemma}[Exact projection and worst-case lower bound]
\label{thm:observation-arity}
Two downward-closed co-liveness families with equal
$\Pi_{\le r_e}$ have the same history-admission verdict.  Conversely, for
every $k\ge1$, the family
\[
U(k,k{+}1)=
\{X\subseteq\{d_0,\ldots,d_k\}\mid |X|\le k\}
\]
has a safe and an unsafe controller realization with identical access,
controller-local families, identities, and $\Pi_{\le k}$ observations.
Only the unsafe realization permits all $k{+}1$ controllers jointly.  Hence no
arity-$k$ checker is exact over all downward-closed realizations; here
$r_e=k{+}1$.
\end{lemma}

The boundary/full-simplex collision is classical
~\cite{vanglabbeek2009configuration,emtander2010hypergraphs,
wang2024hypergraph}.  The mechanized result lifts it through controller-cover
realizability and required coverage.

\begin{proposition}[Independent identity erasures]
\label{prop:identity-erasure}
There is one fixed two-handle, two-gate raw manifest whose four normalized
worlds satisfy
\[
\mathsf{Ready}_M(q_{\mathrm{cell}}^x,q_{\mathrm{ctrl}}^y)
\Longleftrightarrow x={=}\lor y={=},
\qquad x,y\in\{=,\ne\}.
\]
Keeping the raw manifest and either quotient while erasing the other therefore
equates a ready world with the nonready $(\ne,\ne)$ world.
\end{proposition}

Together with two further mechanized collisions, this yields the complete
evidence claim:
\begin{corollary}[Worst-case tight history-admission evidence]
\label{cor:evidence-boundary}
The tuple
\[
(e,\mathcal A,D,q_{\rm cell},\ell,q_{\rm ctrl},
E,L,\Pi_{\le r_e}(\Gamma))
\]
is sufficient for the exact structural verdict.  In the model class, no exact
verdict can uniformly erase any of the following semantic distinctions:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=1pt]
  \item \emph{edge mode}: identical arms under choice Fork are admissible,
  whereas parallel Fork requires their forbidden joint future;
  \item \emph{durable frontier}: the same restored cell is fresh at $D$ and
  stale after a receipt for its lineage atom grows the frontier;
  \item \emph{cell identity}: aliases of one atomic redemption cell and two
  independently redeemable cells can have opposite verdicts;
  \item \emph{controller identity}: one shared controller and two independently
  live controllers can have opposite verdicts; and
  \item \emph{co-liveness arity}: the $U(k,k{+}1)$ collision rules out every
  uniformly smaller projection bound.
\end{enumerate}
\end{corollary}

The claim is semantic irredundancy, not uniqueness of representation:
equivalent evidence may encode a distinction differently, concrete $E,L$ can
lower the required arity, and a fail-closed checker can reject with less
information.

The append-only commitment model additionally proves that fresh \Prepare{}
steps, and only those steps, grow the durable atom frontier; Replay, Retry,
Crash, and Settle do not mint a second receipt.  This mechanized substrate
justifies successive $D$ values.  It does not prove external exactly-once
effects or the atomic installation bridge.  The central theorem is therefore
an exact conditional statement: it specifies the evidence and refinement
obligations a concrete Agent runtime must discharge rather than treating
adapter attestations as proved facts.
