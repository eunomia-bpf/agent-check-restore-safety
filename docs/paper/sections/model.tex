\section{Agent History Semantics}
\label{sec:model}

The model is finite at one history cut but permits an unbounded sequence of
cuts.  One history state covers one authority-policy domain; domains with
disjoint cells, logs, and policies compose as a product.  Internal reasoning, model
calls, and speculative file edits are unrestricted.  Before a protected call
can become durable, its occurrence must belong to the current registered
contract.

\subsection{Causal-completion contracts}

A \emph{logical protected occurrence} \(x\) is a unique invocation slot, not
an effect identity.  The protected-call registry authenticates its gate and
handle certificate and maps it to a semantic redemption cell
\(q_{\rm cell}(x)=d\) and authority label \(\ell(d)\).  Different occurrences
may map to the same cell after Fork or Restore.

A pomset \(p=(X_p,\prec_p)\) is a finite set of occurrences with a strict
partial order.  Its linearizations \(\Lin(p)\) are the words containing each
member of \(X_p\) once and respecting \(\prec_p\).  A
\emph{causal-completion contract}
\(\mathcal C=(O,\{p_o\}_{o\in O})\) is a finite nonempty
\emph{indexed} family of pomsets.  Index \(o\) is a complete outcome identity;
it is retained even if two residual pomsets later become equal.  Write
\(X_{\mathcal C}=\bigcup_{o\in O}X_{p_o}\) and
\(\mathsf{CL}(\mathcal C)=\bigcup_{o\in O}\Lin(p_o)\).  We require
\emph{completion observability}:
\[
 v,w\in\mathsf{CL}(\mathcal C)\ \land\
 v\preceq_{\rm pref}w \quad\Longrightarrow\quad v=w .
\]
Thus one complete trace cannot be a strict prefix of another.  This is not
outcome determinism: incomparable traces and different indices with the same
trace remain valid.  This condition fixes the interface boundary used here:
whether an Agent stops or continues at a shared prefix must be represented by
a registered logical occurrence, rather than inferred from silence.  The
following constructors are partial and are defined only when their result remains
completion-observable:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:contract-algebra}
\begin{aligned}
 O_{\mathcal C\oplus\mathcal D}
  &= (\{L\}\mathbin{\times}O_{\mathcal C})
     \uplus(\{R\}\mathbin{\times}O_{\mathcal D}),\\
 p_{(L,o)}&=p_o,\qquad p_{(R,v)}=p_v,\\
 O_{\mathcal C\otimes\mathcal D}
  &=O_{\mathcal C}\mathbin{\times}O_{\mathcal D},\\
 p_{(o,v)}&=p_o\uplus p_v,\\
 O_{\mathcal C\triangleright\mathcal D}
  &=O_{\mathcal C}\mathbin{\times}O_{\mathcal D},\\
 p_{(o,v)}&=p_o\triangleright p_v.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
Every binary constructor has occurrence-disjoint operands.  Before composing
a newly registered or cloned operand, the history judgment deterministically
freshens its logical occurrence identifiers and extends \(\rho\), while
preserving authenticated cell identities.  Existing carried operands are
already disjoint by history well-formedness.
The parallel product \(\uplus\) adds no cross-order; the ordered product
\(\triangleright\) places every event of \(p\) before every event of \(q\).
The index set of \(\oplus\) is a tagged disjoint union; those of
\(\otimes\) and \(\triangleright\) are Cartesian products.  Thus choice
preserves an outcome from each arm, parallel requires both arms but permits
any causal linearization, and sequence adds a barrier.

For a raw prefix \(w\), the residual \(\mathcal C/w\) retains every index
\(o\) for which \(w\) is a linearization prefix of \(p_o\), and removes the
occurrences in \(w\) with their incident order.  It is undefined when no index
remains.  This derivative gives an operational meaning to ``remaining
contract'' without erasing either outcome or occurrence identity.

\begin{lemma}[Observable completion]
\label{lem:observable-completion}
Every defined residual \(\mathcal C/w\) is completion-observable, and either
all of its retained pomsets are empty or none is.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
If residual suffix \(v\) strictly prefixes \(v'\), then \(wv\) strictly
prefixes \(wv'\) in \(\mathsf{CL}(\mathcal C)\).  If one retained pomset is
empty and another is not, choose its nonempty suffix \(v\); then \(w\)
strictly prefixes \(wv\).  Both contradict completion observability.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Trusted history state}

An Agent history state is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:history-state}
 H=(\omega,G,T,K,\Sigma_\zeta,\rho,\beta,\chi,\nu,\eta).
\end{equation}
\(\omega\) is the policy-domain identity and \(\eta\) its single current
controller epoch.  \(G\) is a finite append-only provenance DAG.  Each
continuation node \(b\) carries immutable registered base contract
\(\Gamma_b\) and branch-local resolved cursor \(\chi_b\); its edges record
typed Fork, Restore, and Merge operations.
\(T\) is the structured live frontier:
\[
 T ::= b{:}\mathcal C
     \mid T\oplus_g^s T
     \mid T\parallel_g T
     \mid \mathsf{jbar}(T,T)
     \mid T;T .
\]
\(s\in\{\mathsf{open},L,R\}\) records whether a choice is unresolved or which
arm has made durable logical progress.  Group identifier \(g\) records
exclusive or jointly live siblings.  The contract interpretation
\(\sem{T}\) uses both alternatives when \(s=\mathsf{open}\), only the selected
residual afterward, \(\otimes\) for parallel and \(\mathsf{jbar}\), and
\(\triangleright\) for sequence.
The unaddressed constructor \(\mathsf{jbar}\) records that a parallel group
has been joined and therefore cannot be matched by another Merge.

A frontier \(T\) is \(\mathsf{complete}\) exactly when every pomset in
\(\sem{T}\) is empty; \cref{lem:observable-completion} rules out a mixture of
empty and nonempty active outcomes.  We set
\(\mathsf{heads}(b{:}\mathcal C)=\{b\}\); choice exposes both arms while open
and only its selected arm afterward; and parallel and \(\mathsf{jbar}\)
expose the union of their arms.  Sequence exposes its left operand until it
is complete, then its right.  Here \(\mathsf{heads}(T_1,T_2)\) abbreviates
the union.  Occurrence progress residualizes leaf contracts but never deletes
frontier structure; sequence exposure is predicate driven, so completed
left-outcome indices remain in \(\sem{T}\).  In particular, an isolated
completed leaf is not collapsed: \(b\) remains addressable for a later Fork
or Restore.
Consequently a joined group loses \(g\) immediately, while its continuation
remains behind a barrier until both arms complete.
An occurrence is enabled exactly when it is minimal in some pomset of
\(\sem{T}\); observable completion makes this agree with sequence exposure.
Let \(\mathsf{addr}(T)\) contain the branch and group identifiers reached by
the same exposure recursion: an exposed group node is included before
descending into its live arm(s), and a sequence descends only into its exposed
operand.  Thus a dormant loser and an unexposed sequence operand are not
addressable.

\(K\) maps checkpoint identifiers to immutable earlier continuation records;
write \(\mathcal C_k=\mathsf{contract}(K(k))\) for the residual contract
stored by checkpoint \(k\).  \(\Sigma_\zeta\) is a versioned immutable
registry of typed edit schemas.
\(\rho\) contains the authenticated occurrence-to-gate/cell, handle,
lineage, scope, and effect-digest data.  For each live occurrence \(x\), binding
\(\beta(x)=(j,\eta)\) names its gate \(j\) and the domain's current epoch.
Finally, \(\chi\) is the ordered append-only trace of durably
\emph{resolved} logical occurrences, including \Fresh and \Alias bindings,
and \(\nu\) is the history-view version.  Resolution may still await remote
settlement or return; \(\chi\) records authorization/causal progress, not
physical completion.

\begin{definition}[Well-formed history]
\label{def:well-formed-history}
\(H\) is well formed when:
(i) \(G\) is acyclic; continuation and group identifiers are globally unique
across the recorded DAG and live frontier; every addressable identifier has a
unique context decomposition; and
every head of \(T\) is a live maximal node;
(ii) every checkpoint names an immutable node and contract in \(G\);
(iii) occurrence and gate identifiers are unique, while cell aliases agree on
issuer, scope, digest, and lineage;
(iv) resolved occurrences are downward closed in their pomset, consistent
with every choice status, and absent from the residual frontier; and
(v) every live gate has exactly one binding \((j,\eta)\) to the domain's
single current epoch; and
(vi) every live leaf \(b{:}\mathcal C\) is exactly the indexed residual of
its immutable registered base \(\Gamma_b\) by the raw projection of its
branch-local suffix \(\chi_b\), rather than a caller-pruned subfamily; and
(vii) every constructor in \(T\) is defined, hence every subtree
interpretation is completion-observable; and
(viii) every edge and introduced contract is justified by one immutable
schema at version \(\zeta\); and
(ix) every current/checkpoint/schema occurrence, cell, gate, lineage, and
scope belongs to \(\omega\).  Cells, logs, and policies of distinct domains
are disjoint; an edit never crosses a domain.
The durable kernel additionally requires that the one current domain epoch is
active.
\end{definition}

The ledger state is not part of \(H\).  It stores a policy version \(\kappa\),
a monotone durable receipt ledger \(\Delta\), and an ordered outbox.  Receipt
creation records are append only; their phase and stable result future may
advance monotonically when the remote result settles.  Each receipt binds its
cell, creating invocation, and authority label.  Every resolved
record in \(\chi\) points to that receipt: directly for \Fresh and to the
earlier receipt for \Alias.  Thus same-invocation retry and alias result lookup
are functions of \((\chi,\Delta)\), not unstated volatile tables.
\(\mathsf{lab}(\Delta)\) is its authority-event trace.  Restore may reconstruct
\(H\)'s workspace payload, but no member of \(\Delta\) is restored.

A security state
\((\kappa,H,\Delta,\mathsf{out},\mathcal A)\) is \emph{well formed} when
\(H\) is well formed, \(\mathsf{lab}(\Delta)\in\mathcal A\), each semantic
cell has at most one fresh receipt, every fresh (respectively alias) record
in \(\chi\) names its unique (respectively an earlier) receipt, and the
log, outbox, and policy are scoped to \(\omega\), and the outbox preserves the
order of fresh records.  This joint invariant, rather
than history well-formedness alone, is assumed below.

\subsection{Six structural rewrite rules}

An untrusted request contains a mode, live object identifiers, and an edit
schema identifier \(\sigma\), never a target contract.  Registry
\(\Sigma_\zeta\) binds \(\sigma\) to its operation kind, exact source and
checkpoint fingerprints, introduced suffix contracts, and a lineage
certificate.

The certificate makes ``history-derived'' checkable.  For indexed source
\(\mathcal C\), one schema-designated carried or cloned target region
\(\mathcal D\), and parent map \(\mu:X_{\mathcal D}\to X_{\mathcal C}\),
write \(\mathcal C\preccurlyeq_\mu\mathcal D\) when \(\mu\) is a global
bijection and there is a surjection
\(\pi:O_{\mathcal D}\twoheadrightarrow O_{\mathcal C}\) such that, for every
target outcome \(v\),
\[
\begin{aligned}
 x\in X_{p_v}
   &\Longleftrightarrow \mu(x)\in X_{p_{\pi(v)}},\\
 x\prec_v y
   &\Longleftrightarrow \mu(x)\prec_{\pi(v)}\mu(y),
\end{aligned}
\]
and \(\mu\) preserves cell identity and lineage.  Global bijection plus the
incidence equivalence prevents one source occurrence shared across outcomes
from being split into outcome-specific target occurrences.

For a candidate target \(T'\), a rewrite certificate is a family
\(\mathcal P_u=\{(\mathcal C_r,\mathcal D_r,\mu_r,\pi_r)\}_{r\in R_u}\)
of these relations over disjoint target regions inside the replaced subtree.
The exposed one-hole context is checked separately by the identity map: its
syntax, outcome indices, occurrences, cells, lineage, and order are unchanged.
Let \(X_{\rm ctx}\) be the identity-preserved context occurrences and
\(X_{\rm suf(u)}\) the occurrences in the schema-declared suffixes.  The
checker verifies the disjoint cover
\[
 X_{\sem{T'}}=X_{\rm ctx}\uplus
   \biguplus_{r\in R_u}X_{\mathcal D_r}\uplus X_{\rm suf(u)} .
\]
The row constructor and exposed-context syntax induce a partial outcome
projection
\(\mathsf{pr}^u_r:O_{\sem{T'}}\rightharpoonup O_{\mathcal D_r}\):
choice is defined only in the arm containing region \(r\), while product and
sequence select the corresponding indexed component.  Define
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \mathsf{Obl}_u&=\{(r,o)\mid r\in R_u,\ o\in O_{\mathcal C_r}\},\\
 \mathsf{Lift}_u(t,r,o)&\Longleftrightarrow
   \mathsf{pr}^u_r(t)=v\ \land\ \pi_r(v)=o .
\end{aligned}
\]
The checker requires
\(\forall(r,o)\in\mathsf{Obl}_u.\ \exists t\in O_{\sem{T'}}.\
\mathsf{Lift}_u(t,r,o)\).  Context alternatives bypassing the hole retain
their full outcome index by identity.  Hence every required regional source
outcome has a full-target lift, no shared occurrence is split by outcome, and
no inherited occurrence is mislabeled as a suffix.

The schema checker accepts
\[
\Sigma_\zeta;H\vdash_{\rm edit}u\Rightarrow(T',\mathcal P_u)
\]
only when every named live object lies in \(\mathsf{addr}(T)\), every
introduced object remains scoped to \(\omega\), the source
fingerprint matches, the appropriate
\(\mathcal P_u\), disjoint-cover, and \(\mathsf{Lift}_u\) obligations in
\cref{tab:history-rules} hold, any
retirement is authorized, and \(\sem{T'}\) is defined.  Retirement is limited
to the losing arm of a typed \(\mathsf{MergeSelect}\), or the current
continuation of \(\mathsf{RestoreReplace}\) when it is complete or
\(\sigma\) contains an explicit retirement certificate.  These are checked
edge facts, not Agent declarations.  A retirement certificate is an
issuer-authenticated immutable record at version \(\zeta\) binding
\((\omega,\sigma)\), the exact source fingerprint, and the exact retired
outcome indices and occurrence lineages.  The checker verifies that no
occurrence outside that set disappears.  For MergeSelect the certified set
must equal the typed losing arm; for RestoreReplace it must equal the current
continuation.  Treating that explicitly authorized set as discharged is a
trusted schema decision, not a theorem that the retirement was desirable.

Let \(\mathcal E[-]\) be a one-hole \emph{exposed} frontier context, so its
hole has the addressability property above.
\(\mathsf{clone}\) creates fresh continuation, occurrence, and gate identities
while preserving authenticated cells and lineage; \(\mathsf{carry}\)
preserves remaining occurrences and cells.  The schema-derived contracts
\(E_\sigma^L,E_\sigma^R,E_\sigma^J\) are suffixes, not replacements.  Define
\[
 L_\sigma=\mathsf{clone}_L(\mathcal C)\triangleright E_\sigma^L,\qquad
 R_\sigma=\mathsf{clone}_R(\mathcal C)\triangleright E_\sigma^R .
\]
Fresh identifiers and the target epoch \(\eta^+\) are deterministically
allocated from \((\omega,\zeta,\nu,u,\mathsf{role})\).
Write \(\mathsf{Cl}_r(\mathcal C)\) for the certified tuple
\((\mathcal C,\mathsf{clone}_r(\mathcal C),\mu_r,\pi_r)\), and
\(\mathsf{Ca}(\mathcal C)\) for
\((\mathcal C,\mathsf{carry}(\mathcal C),\mu,\pi)\); each abbreviation asserts
the corresponding \(\preccurlyeq_\mu\) relation.  For a frontier \(T_0\),
\(\mathsf{Ca}(T_0)\) abbreviates \(\mathsf{Ca}(\sem{T_0})\) and has target contract
\(\sem{\mathsf{carry}(T_0)}\).  Every \(b\) or \(g\) named below must lie in
\(\mathsf{addr}(T)\).

The first fresh or alias occurrence in an open choice atomically sets its
status; later occurrences from the other arm are disabled.  Hence
\(\mathsf{MergeSelect}(g,w,\sigma)\) has no derivation after durable logical
progress in the loser, even when that progress was receipt silent.
In \(\mathsf{MergeJoin}\), replacing the addressable
\(\parallel_g\) node by \(\mathsf{jbar}\) retires \(g\) in the same rewrite;
the same pair therefore cannot be joined twice.

Handoff is deliberately domain-wide rather than presented as a selective
owner analysis.  Every live gate in \(H\) belongs to \(\eta^-=\eta\); every
gate in \(T'\), including unchanged context, is rebound to the single fresh
\(\eta^+\).  The complete judgment is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:derived-cut}
\begin{aligned}
 \Sigma_\zeta;H\vdash u\Downarrow
   &(H',\mathcal C_{H,u},\eta^-,\eta^+),\\
\mathcal C_{H,u}&=\sem{T'},\qquad
\eta^-=\eta .
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
\(G'\) appends the typed edge and target nodes; retired nodes remain in
\(G'\), \(\nu'=\nu+1\), \(\eta'=\eta^+\), and every target binding names
\(\eta^+\).  A request naming a nonexistent checkpoint, wrong group mode,
nonmember winner, mismatched schema fingerprint, unauthorized retirement, or
undefined composition has no derivation.  Independent domains step by
asynchronous product; no theorem below claims within-domain partial fencing.

\begin{lemma}[Schema fidelity and epoch totality]
\label{lem:history-derivation}
For well-formed \(H\), the judgment in \cref{eq:derived-cut} is deterministic.
If it derives \(H'\), then \(H'\) is well formed.
No pending source lineage disappears except through an authorized retirement;
the identity-preserved context and family \(\mathcal P_u\) preserve global
occurrence sharing, cell, lineage, and causal order.  For every
\((r,o)\in\mathsf{Obl}_u\), some full target outcome \(t\) satisfies
\(\mathsf{Lift}_u(t,r,o)\); a context outcome bypassing the hole is preserved
by identity.  Every live target gate is bound to
\(\eta^+\), and no target gate remains bound to \(\eta^-\).
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Unique addressable object and schema identifiers select at most one table row
and one schema record.  The exposed-context identity check preserves
everything outside the hole.  Both Fork rules certify the two clone regions;
the choice or product constructor lifts their outcomes appropriately.
RestoreReplace maps
the checkpoint clone and checks retirement of the current source; RestoreLive
certifies the carried source and checkpoint clone.  MergeSelect certifies the
winner and retires only the typed loser; MergeJoin certifies its two carry
regions before removing \(g\) through the unaddressed barrier.  Clone/carry
construction gives global bijection and incidence preservation in each case,
and structural induction over the displayed constructor and exposed context
defines \(\mathsf{pr}^u_r\), proves the disjoint cover, and supplies each
required \(\mathsf{Lift}_u\) witness.
Fresh target identifiers preserve
acyclicity and uniqueness; \cref{lem:observable-completion} and the partial
constructor checks preserve contract well-formedness.  Finally, the
domain-wide rebinding maps every live gate to the one deterministically
derived \(\eta^+\), establishing epoch totality.
\end{proof}

This lemma rules out the oracle used by a caller-described plan.  A Fork
cannot replace pending \(\mathcal C\) with two harmless registered contracts:
both schema-derived arms must project onto \(\mathcal C\).  A Join cannot
pretend that one input is complete because the rule reads both residuals from
\(T\), and \(E_\sigma^J\) can only extend them.

\subsection{Adversary and trusted boundary}

The adversary controls Agent output, operation choice, tool arguments, copied
workspace state, retries, stale seals, and scheduling.  It may race any live
protected call against a history edit.  It cannot forge the protected-call
or edit-schema registries, checkpoint/DAG store, cell certificates, policy,
or durable ledger.  We assume complete mediation and one linearizable,
crash-stable transaction per policy domain for logical occurrence resolution
or epoch installation.  This is an explicit reference-monitor TCB, not a
claim to implement a new storage protocol.  The model neither infers a
contract from natural language nor claims that deployed Codex or Claude
currently supplies this trusted state.
