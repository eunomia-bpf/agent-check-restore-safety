\section{Related Work}
% 相关工作
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Recovery and rollback}
% 恢复与回滚
Output-commit recovery delays effects until state commits and reconstructs a fixed computation after failure.
% 输出提交恢复将效果延迟到状态提交后，并在故障后重建固定计算。
It does not decide whether an Agent may safely change its branch and checkpoint structure while several outcomes share one-use permissions~\cite{strom1985optimistic,elnozahy2002rollback}.
% 它不判定 Agent 能否在多个结果共享一次性权限时安全改变其分支和检查点结构~\cite{strom1985optimistic,elnozahy2002rollback}。

\paragraph{Supervisory control and synthesis}
% 监督控制与综合
Generalized nonblocking starts from a supplied transition system, called the plant, and supplied completion conditions, called markings.
% 广义非阻塞从给定的转移系统（称为被控对象）和给定的完成条件（称为标记）出发。
Live Synthesis similarly starts from an old specification and an execution~\cite{dequeiroz2005multitasking,malik2008generalised,finkbeiner2022live}.
% Live Synthesis 类似地从旧规范和一次执行出发~\cite{dequeiroz2005multitasking,malik2008generalised,finkbeiner2022live}。
Our checker instead derives the edited workflow, every required outcome, and the necessary-and-sufficient safety condition for all six edits from the authenticated execution record before installation.
% 相比之下，我们的检查器在安装之前从认证执行记录中推导编辑后的工作流、每个要求实现的结果，以及全部六种编辑的必要且充分安全条件。

\paragraph{Agent recovery mechanisms}
% Agent 恢复机制
ACRFence blocks replayed effects after Restore, and DART selects checkpoints that preserve earlier work.
% ACRFence 阻止 Restore 后重放效果，DART 选择能够保留先前工作的检查点。
Atomix delays settlement until its required branch boundary, while Rebound makes rollback atomic and auditable~\cite{zheng2026acrfence,yang2026dart,mohammadi2026atomix,burke2026rebound}.
% Atomix 将结算推迟到其要求的分支边界，而 Rebound 使回滚具有原子性与可审计性~\cite{zheng2026acrfence,yang2026dart,mohammadi2026atomix,burke2026rebound}。
These mechanisms take logs, scopes, call classes, branch boundaries, or policies as inputs rather than deriving the full safety condition of an execution edit from its authenticated record.
% 这些机制将日志、作用域、调用类别、分支边界或策略作为输入，而不是从执行的认证记录中推导一次执行编辑的完整安全条件。
