\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Safety monitors and Agent authorization}
Execution monitors enforce prefix-closed policies by mediating
security-relevant actions~\cite{schneider2000enforceable}.  Consumable
credentials provide atomic durable ratification
~\cite{bowers2007consumable}; Commit-Time Authorization binds freshness,
order, and eligibility to effect release~\cite{santos2026committime}; and
FORGE evaluates policies over an Agent's causal-history view
~\cite{palumbo2026forge}.  Under complete serialized mediation these systems
can form the strong $\mathsf{GM}$ baseline used here.  Our result does not
claim better per-action safety.  It gives a typed history-cut semantics,
compiles the resulting causal residual into an epoch, and proves that an
atomic live replacement preserves the reference language under races.

\paragraph{Automata and supervisory control}
Residual languages, canonical deterministic automata, and language
equivalence are classical~\cite{brzozowski1964derivatives}.  Supervisory
control synthesizes legal sublanguages under controllability and observation
constraints~\cite{ramadge1987supervisory,cieslak1988partial}; range control
adds lower and upper language bounds~\cite{yin2017range}; and decentralized
supervision/localization distribute a monolithic supervisor while preserving
controlled behavior~\cite{komenda2017decentralized,
zhang2017localization}.  Reconfigurable DES work introduces explicit events
for moving between already modeled configurations
~\cite{macktoobian2022reconfiguration}.

We use the first of these results to represent $F_R$ and deliberately emit one
logical epoch rather than claiming a new separability criterion.  The
Agent-specific theorem begins one level earlier and ends one level later: a
raw Fork/Restore/Merge cut plus copied identities and an irreversible receipt
trace derives the language, while a version/frontier CAS retires the old
language in the presence of concurrent fresh effects.  Removing either side
reduces our construction to established automata or supervisor machinery.

\paragraph{Causality, choice, and rollback}
Event and configuration structures represent causality, conflict, and
concurrency~\cite{winskel1987event,vanglabbeek2009configuration}; workflow and
separation models provide related composition foundations
~\cite{meyer2018workflow,pym2004bi}.  Our plan grammar uses only finite
sequence, tagged choice, and shuffle, and makes no claim to a new model of
concurrency.  Its purpose is to prevent the security reduction from collapsing
approval-before-payment, exclusive alternatives, and join prerequisites to
the same endpoint set.

Rollback recovery reconstructs a consistent past and treats output commit as
a boundary beyond recoverable computation~\cite{elnozahy2002rollback}.
Memoir, ROTE, and LCM preserve or detect continuity, rollback, and forks
~\cite{parno2011memoir,matetic2017rote,brandenburger2017lcm}.  These protect
past state.  A live Agent cut additionally needs a future controller program
and a linearization point between old and new commit eligibility; that is the
role of the canonical epoch and \textsc{Install}.

\paragraph{Agent lifecycle and effect substrates}
ACRFence exposes authority resurrection after restore, while DART checks
whether downstream commitments leave a checkpoint recoverable
~\cite{zheng2026acrfence,yang2026dart}.  Crab, Fork--Explore--Commit, and
Agent libOS provide concrete branching, checkpoint, selection, and
capability-controlled substrates~\cite{wu2026crab,wang2026fork,
zhang2026agentlibos}.  PORTICO compiles task contracts into epoch-bound
capabilities, and Earned Authority bounds effects across Agent mutation
~\cite{santos2026lingering,zhang2026earned}.  Atomix and Cordon stage
multi-tool effects~\cite{mohammadi2026atomix,chen2026cordon}; transactional
outboxes and stable idempotency keys are possible durable substrates
~\cite{aws2026outbox,aws2026idempotency}.

These mechanisms can implement anchors, receipts, fencing, or effect release.
They do not give the theorem proved here: exact causal monitor residualization
for six online history cuts plus a race-safe replacement rule that closes every
source epoch.  Conversely, our abstract kernel does not establish their
parsers, mediation, crash recovery, or external exactly-once delivery.

\paragraph{Coordination and hyperproperties}
Invariant confluence and Complete CALM characterize when coordination can be
avoided for broader classes of state or history specifications
~\cite{bailis2014coordination,hellerstein2026completecalm}.  Runtime
enforcement and synthesis for hyperproperties relate several traces
~\cite{clarkson2008hyperproperties,bonakdarpour2020controller,
coenen2022hyperproperties}.  We claim neither a universal coordination
criterion nor a new hyperproperty formalism.  The cross-history issue here is
compiled into one finite causal safety language at each Agent cut; the new
obligation is to replace its authoritative epoch without admitting a
cross-version trace.
