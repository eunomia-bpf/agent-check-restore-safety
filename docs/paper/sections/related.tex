\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Checkpointing, rollback, and output commit}
Rollback-recovery work reconstructs a consistent past and identifies output
commit as the boundary at which effects escape recoverable computation
~\cite{elnozahy2002rollback}.  Memoir, ROTE, and LCM protect continuity or
detect rollback and forks
~\cite{parno2011memoir,matetic2017rote,brandenburger2017lcm}; Rebound makes
authorized rollback append-only~\cite{burke2026rebound}.  We derive the
correlated future admissible when a typed operation retains or joins
continuations; we propose no snapshot, rollback, or output-commit protocol.

\paragraph{Configurations, conflict, and minimal obstructions}
Event structures already represent causality, conflict, and valid
configurations, while BI, fork/join separation logics, and workflow models
account for resource composition~\cite{winskel1987event,pym2004bi,
moine2024dislog,meyer2018workflow}; graph projections can also lose
higher-order structure~\cite{wang2024hypergraph}.  Here a typed edge generates
candidate/required families, receipts filter the candidate, and an independent
controller product is checked against the result.  Minimal nonfaces become
deployment counterexamples only after excluding outside-support and locally
forbidden choices.

\paragraph{Capabilities, linear resources, and consumable authority}
Consumable credentials combine linear authorization with durable ratification
so copied proof material cannot productively reuse bounded authority
~\cite{bowers2007consumable}.  Capability calculi, resource-aware session
types, and architectures also split, bound, delegate, and revoke resources
~\cite{terauchi2006capability,das2018resource,yu2023capstone}.  They can
implement one redemption cell and prevent alias double-spend.  Our cover
theorem instead checks forbidden unions of distinct fresh cells after cell and
controller identities transform independently.

\paragraph{Transactions, coordination, and invariant confluence}
Transactional outboxes and stable idempotency keys couple local intent with
eventual delivery and deduplicate retries
~\cite{aws2026outbox,aws2026idempotency}.  Atomix stages and settles agent tool
effects across resource frontiers, while Cordon constructs semantic
transactions over task lineage, state, effects, and delegated authority
~\cite{mohammadi2026atomix,chen2026cordon}.  Invariant confluence and Complete
CALM characterize when coordination is unnecessary
~\cite{bailis2014coordination,hellerstein2026completecalm}.  We decide one
fixed-prefix edge realization between required and admitted futures, not a
universal coordination criterion or unique finest cover.

\paragraph{Supervisory enforcement and certifying compilation}
Supervisory control synthesizes legal behavior under controllability and
observation assumptions, including partial/relative observation and structured
processes
~\cite{ramadge1987supervisory,cieslak1988partial,cai2015relative,ma2006sts,feng2007ctp}.
Decentralized control studies local controllers jointly enforcing a
specification~\cite{komenda2017decentralized}; security automata and runtime
hyperproperty enforcement mediate trace and cross-trace policies
~\cite{schneider2000enforceable,coenen2022hyperproperties}.  Range control
already combines upper legal and lower required languages with maximally
permissive synthesis~\cite{yin2017range}.  Our corollary only prunes submitted
$\Gamma$ at fixed $E,L$ after the edge and prefix derive its bounds; the
combined boundary and observation theorem are not general supervisor or
hyperproperty synthesis.

\paragraph{Agent checkpoint and branching substrates}
ACRFence identifies replay and authority resurrection after restore
~\cite{zheng2026acrfence}; DART checks checkpoint recoverability after
downstream commitments~\cite{yang2026dart}.
Crab, Fork--Explore--Commit, and Agent libOS provide branching/checkpoint
substrates~\cite{wu2026crab,wang2026fork,zhang2026agentlibos}.  We type six
history edges and check commitments against receipts/controllers; we claim
neither checkpoint fidelity nor an unmodified adapter.

\paragraph{History-sensitive agent policy and evolving authority}
FORGE monitors Datalog policies over causal history under an observation
contract~\cite{palumbo2026forge}.  We instead generate one correlated family
from a typed edge, filter it by receipts, and check its controller product.

Hu and Wang show that locally benign agent fragments may assemble into harm
that no local-view monitor detects~\cite{hu2026localmonitors}.  Our lower bound
distinguishes two $U(k,k{+}1)$ co-liveness families with equal arity-$k$
projections.  Unlike $k$-safety's trace witnesses and self-composition
~\cite{clarkson2008hyperproperties}, it observes controller sets and includes
fixed-$E,L$ realizability and required coverage; cover compression supplies the
upper bound.

Earned Authority fixes an effect ceiling across agent mutation
~\cite{zhang2026earned}, while PORTICO compiles task contracts into epoch-bound
capabilities~\cite{santos2026lingering}.  We ask which correlations survive a
typed edge and whether controller composition exceeds them.

\paragraph{Commit authorization and external effects}
Commit-Time Authorization checks one effect witness at commit
~\cite{santos2026committime}; Ghost Tool Calls studies issue-time disclosure
~\cite{mohammadi2026ghost}; Atomix and Cordon provide transactional effect
handling~\cite{mohammadi2026atomix,chen2026cordon}.  Fresh individual effects
may still be forbidden together, while our seal neither authorizes one effect
nor prevents pre-commit disclosure.

\paragraph{Combined claim boundary}
The novelty is the combined decision boundary: typed edges generate futures,
receipts select the greatest admitted subfamily, independent cell/controller
normalizations induce a product, and a checker emits evidence, a greatest
co-liveness-only proposal, or a typed failure using the
untrusted-producer/small-checker pattern~\cite{necula1997pcc}.  The
contract-indexed upper and arbitrary-arity lower bounds characterize truncated
co-liveness evidence.  Under
$\mathsf{Required}\subseteq\mathsf{Actual}
\subseteq\mathsf{RawPhysical}$, the certificate transports to runtime behavior.
