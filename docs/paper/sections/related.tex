\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Checkpointing, rollback, and output commit.}
Rollback-recovery work reconstructs a consistent past and identifies output
commit as the boundary at which effects escape recoverable computation
~\cite{elnozahy2002rollback}.  Memoir protects module state continuity with a
trusted history summary, ROTE distributes rollback protection, and LCM detects
rollback and adversarial forks
~\cite{parno2011memoir,matetic2017rote,brandenburger2017lcm}.  Rebound instead
makes an authorized rollback an append-only transition in one authenticated
state history~\cite{burke2026rebound}.  We rely on the same principle that a
snapshot cannot rewind security history.  Our question begins after that
boundary is fixed: a typed operation may intentionally retain several
continuations or join them, so admission must derive the correlated future
that remains compatible with the durable past.  We do not propose a new
snapshot algorithm, rollback protocol, or output-commit solution.

\paragraph{Configurations, conflict, and minimal obstructions.}
Event structures already represent causality, conflict, and valid
configurations, while BI, fork/join separation logics, and workflow models
provide established accounts of resource composition and concurrency
~\cite{winskel1987event,pym2004bi,moine2024dislog,meyer2018workflow}.  Our
downward-closed future families, choice/tensor operators, and use of minimal
nonfaces build on this vocabulary.  We make no mathematical novelty claim for
configuration families or minimal nonfaces themselves.  The contribution is
their placement in a security decision with an immutable receipt prefix: the
typed history operator generates both candidate and required families, lineage
filters that candidate, and an independent controller product is then checked
against the resulting admitted family.  Minimal nonfaces serve as compact
deployment counterexamples only after outside-support and locally forbidden
choices have been ruled out.

\paragraph{Capabilities, linear resources, and consumable authority.}
Consumable credentials combine linear authorization with durable ratification
so copied proof material cannot productively reuse bounded authority
~\cite{bowers2007consumable}.  Capability calculi, resource-aware session
types, and capability architectures likewise reason about splitting,
transferring, bounding, delegating, and revoking resources
~\cite{terauchi2006capability,das2018resource,yu2023capstone}.  We therefore do
not claim linear tokens, atomic consumption, or stable nonces.  Such mechanisms
can implement one semantic redemption cell and stop two aliases from spending
it twice.  They do not alone exclude a forbidden union of several distinct,
individually fresh cells.  Our additional problem is that redemption-cell
identity and enforcement-controller identity can be transformed independently;
the controller-cover theorem checks the higher-order correlation that remains
after per-cell linearity succeeds.

\paragraph{Transactions, coordination, and invariant confluence.}
Transactional outboxes and stable idempotency keys couple local intent with
eventual delivery and deduplicate retries
~\cite{aws2026outbox,aws2026idempotency}.  Atomix stages and settles agent tool
effects across resource frontiers, while Cordon constructs semantic
transactions over task lineage, state, effects, and delegated authority
~\cite{mohammadi2026atomix,chen2026cordon}.  These mechanisms are plausible
substrates for implementing a history-admission decision; they do not make the
decision itself a transaction theorem.  Invariant-confluence-style analyses
ask when fixed operations can preserve an invariant without coordination
~\cite{bailis2014coordination}.
Our input is different: Fork/Restore/Merge first rewrites the may and required
future, and the compiler then identifies whether the declared controller
product introduces a forbidden union.  In the canonical functional-partition
case the minimal-nonface hypergraph yields mandatory coordination components;
for overlapping access and arbitrary local families we claim neither a unique
finest cover nor a coordination-free execution theorem.

\paragraph{Supervisory enforcement and certifying compilation.}
Supervisory control synthesizes legal behavior under controllability and
observation assumptions; later work studies partial and relative observability,
hierarchical state structures, and guarded transaction processes
~\cite{ramadge1987supervisory,cieslak1988partial,cai2015relative,ma2006sts,feng2007ctp}.
Security-automata-style monitors likewise enforce trace properties at mediated
events~\cite{schneider2000enforceable}.  We do not claim a general maximally
permissive supervisor,
nonblocking control, or a new enforcement-automata formalism.  Our greatest
residual is the simpler complete-information filter inside one fixed,
operator-generated candidate.  The distinct controller-cover layer then asks
whether a manifest's independently composed local choices refine that
residual.

The artifact also follows the familiar certifying pattern of an untrusted
producer and a smaller independent checker~\cite{necula1997pcc}.  This is
trust-structure reuse,
not a claim to proof-carrying code or verified compilation: the checker
reconstructs one finite manifest decision, the Python implementation is not
extracted from Lean, and no theorem connects the JSON parser or an arbitrary
runtime to the formal model.  In particular, a verified seal does not establish
the runtime's $\mathsf{Actual}\subseteq\mathsf{RawPhysical}$ obligation.

\paragraph{Agent checkpoint and branching substrates.}
ACRFence identifies action replay and authority resurrection after agent
restore and proposes a branch-tagged replay-or-fork check at the tool boundary
~\cite{zheng2026acrfence}.  DART checks when a local checkpoint remains
semantically recoverable after downstream commitments~\cite{yang2026dart}.
Crab reconstructs sandbox-visible state; Fork--Explore--Commit supplies
isolated branch contexts and winner selection; and Agent libOS exposes
capability-controlled agent processes and checkpoints
~\cite{wu2026crab,wang2026fork,zhang2026agentlibos}.  Our complementary result
types choice/parallel Fork, replacing/live Restore, and select/join Merge, then
checks the derived future against receipts and controller realization.  It
neither improves checkpoint fidelity nor establishes an unmodified runtime's
adapter obligations.

\paragraph{History-sensitive agent policy and evolving authority.}
FORGE specifies Datalog policies over history predicates maintained by an
observability service with an assume/guarantee contract, and a reference
monitor consults those predicates at policy-relevant actions
~\cite{palumbo2026forge}.  It treats general causal history and observation;
we instead generate one correlated family from a typed edge, filter it by
receipts, and check a co-live controller product.  We do not offer FORGE's
policy language or online-monitor theorem.

Earned Authority fixes a transition envelope and effect ceiling when a grant
is issued, distinguishes requested from realized effects, and conditions
authorization continuity on evidence supplied during later agent mutation
~\cite{zhang2026earned}.  Our result neither replaces nor strengthens that
ceiling: it asks which configurations remain after a typed history edge and
whether the controller layout can exceed them.
PORTICO addresses a complementary temporal gap by compiling task contracts
into epoch-bound resource/effect capabilities and withdrawing them when the
justifying episode closes~\cite{santos2026lingering}.  Such revocable handles
can implement local cell authority; our result concerns correlations among
still-valid cells and does not infer episode closure.

\paragraph{Commit authorization and external effects.}
Commit-Time Authorization checks whether one effect witness remains fresh,
causally prior, target-bound, and eligible when the effect commits
~\cite{santos2026committime}.  Ghost Tool Calls shows that an abandoned
speculative call may disclose intent at issue time, before any later commit
decision~\cite{mohammadi2026ghost}.  Atomix and Cordon address transactional
grouping, staging, recovery, and settlement of agent tool effects
~\cite{mohammadi2026atomix,chen2026cordon}.  These per-effect and transaction
boundaries are orthogonal to our configuration-level question: several effects
may each have a fresh valid witness yet be forbidden from becoming durable
together.  Conversely, our structural admission seal neither authorizes an
individual effect nor prevents issue-time disclosure.

\paragraph{Combined claim boundary.}
No isolated ingredient---durable receipts, typed choice and parallel
composition, greatest safe restriction, minimal nonfaces, linear redemption,
coordination, or independent checking---is our novelty claim.  The combined
result is a decision procedure for a typed history transformation at a fixed
durable frontier.  It normalizes semantic cells independently from controller
instances, derives candidate and required futures, computes the greatest
prefix-compatible continuation, checks the relational controller product, and
emits independently checkable positive evidence or a typed minimal failure
witness.  Subject to
$\mathsf{Required}\subseteq\mathsf{Actual}
\subseteq\mathsf{RawPhysical}$, the admitted prefix certificate then transports
to actual runtime behavior.  This combination, rather than any reused
foundation above, is the paper's claim.
