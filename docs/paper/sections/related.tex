\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{The strongest baseline: a serialized global monitor}
Execution monitors can enforce prefix-closed safety policies by mediating
security-relevant actions~\cite{schneider2000enforceable}.  Consumable
credentials already combine linear authorization with atomic durable
ratification~\cite{bowers2007consumable}; Rebound places authorized rollback
and audit state in one authoritative order~\cite{burke2026rebound};
Commit-Time Authorization checks that one effect witness is still fresh,
bound, ordered, and eligible when the effect becomes durable
~\cite{santos2026committime}.  FORGE is the closest Agent-specific history
monitor: it evaluates Datalog policies over an assumed causal-history view at
policy-relevant actions~\cite{palumbo2026forge}.  Given complete serialized
mediation, such a monitor can reject replay and the final member of a forbidden
combination without preadmitting the Agent edge.  We therefore use it as the
reference semantics, not as a weak baseline.  Our question is when its exact
language can be compiled into the independently live controllers created by a
Fork, Restore, or Merge while retaining every required monitor-legal outcome.

\paragraph{Configuration structures and correlated behavior}
Event and configuration structures already represent causality, conflict, and
higher-order configuration consistency
~\cite{winskel1987event,vanglabbeek2009configuration}.  BI, separation logics,
and workflow models provide further composition foundations
~\cite{pym2004bi,moine2024dislog,meyer2018workflow}.  Downward-closed families,
minimal nonfaces, and the boundary/full-simplex distinction are consequently
classical; low-order projection can lose higher-order structure
~\cite{emtander2010hypergraphs,wang2024hypergraph}.  We use this mathematics
to represent the monitor and controller languages.  The paper's claim is the
Agent-edge derivation, exact compilation equivalence, evolution across durable
frontiers, and tight evidence result---not a new theory of configurations or
minimal obstructions.

\paragraph{Supervisory control, range control, and enforcement}
Supervisory control synthesizes legal sublanguages under controllability and
observation assumptions~\cite{ramadge1987supervisory,cieslak1988partial,
cai2015relative}; decentralized and relative-coobservable variants coordinate
several local supervisors~\cite{cai2019coobservability,
komenda2017decentralized}.  State-tree and transaction-process work already
compiles structured policies into executable supervisors
~\cite{ma2006sts,feng2007ctp}.  Most directly, range control seeks a maximally
permissive language between required and legal bounds
~\cite{yin2017range}; our greatest $\Gamma$ pruning is an instance of this
classical pattern.  Our distinction is that a typed online Agent edge and its
receipts derive both bounds, while the theorem identifies the evidence needed
to compile the serialized monitor for that edge.

\paragraph{Coordination criteria and transactional effects}
Invariant confluence characterizes when fixed database operations can preserve
an invariant without coordination~\cite{bailis2014coordination}; Complete CALM
gives a broader history-based coordination criterion
~\cite{hellerstein2026completecalm}.  We do not claim a universal coordination
criterion.  Our necessity result is conditional and local: if the controller
language for one edge exceeds its global-monitor language, that submitted
detached epoch must be restricted, coordinated, or rejected; if the greatest
fixed-$E,L$ co-liveness restriction loses a required outcome, co-liveness
pruning alone is insufficient.  Transactional outboxes and stable idempotency
keys bind intent durably and deduplicate retries
~\cite{aws2026outbox,aws2026idempotency}; Atomix and Cordon stage and settle
multi-tool Agent effects~\cite{mohammadi2026atomix,chen2026cordon}.  These are
candidate substrates for the theorem's edge-cut and commitment premises.

\paragraph{Rollback, capabilities, and evolving Agent authority}
Rollback recovery reconstructs a consistent past and identifies output commit
as the point beyond recoverable computation~\cite{elnozahy2002rollback}.
Memoir, ROTE, and LCM protect state continuity or detect rollback and forks
~\cite{parno2011memoir,matetic2017rote,brandenburger2017lcm}.  Capability
calculi, resource-aware types, and capability architectures split, delegate,
bound, and revoke authority~\cite{terauchi2006capability,das2018resource,
yu2023capstone}.  Earned Authority proves a fixed effect ceiling across Agent
mutation, while PORTICO compiles task contracts into epoch-bound revocable
capabilities~\cite{zhang2026earned,santos2026lingering}.  These mechanisms
supply essential premises for cell redemption, durable continuity, and
controller fencing.  They do not by themselves derive the correlated
post-edge language or prove that a detached controller epoch both refines the
global monitor and preserves the edge's required future.

\paragraph{Agent checkpoint and branching systems}
ACRFence exposes replay and authority resurrection after restore, and DART
checks whether downstream commitments leave a checkpoint semantically
recoverable~\cite{zheng2026acrfence,yang2026dart}.  Crab,
Fork--Explore--Commit, and Agent libOS provide concrete branching,
checkpointing, selection, and capability-controlled Agent substrates
~\cite{wu2026crab,wang2026fork,zhang2026agentlibos}.  Ghost Tool Calls shows
that abandoned speculative calls may disclose intent before commit
~\cite{mohammadi2026ghost}.  Our six edge relations distinguish exclusive
from co-durable Fork, replacing from live Restore, and select from join Merge.
The theorem concerns durable effect languages after those relations; it
neither supplies checkpoint fidelity nor prevents issue-time disclosure.
