\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Checkpointing, rollback, and output commit}
Rollback-recovery work reconstructs a consistent past and identifies output
commit as the boundary at which effects escape recoverable computation
~\cite{elnozahy2002rollback}.  Memoir, ROTE, and LCM protect continuity or
detect rollback and forks
~\cite{parno2011memoir,matetic2017rote,brandenburger2017lcm}; Rebound makes
authorized rollback append-only~\cite{burke2026rebound}.  We likewise forbid
rewinding security history, but ask what correlated future remains admissible
when a typed operation retains or joins continuations.  We propose no snapshot,
rollback, or output-commit protocol.

\paragraph{Configurations, conflict, and minimal obstructions}
Event structures already represent causality, conflict, and valid
configurations, while BI, fork/join separation logics, and workflow models
account for resource composition~\cite{winskel1987event,pym2004bi,
moine2024dislog,meyer2018workflow}; graph projections can also lose
higher-order structure~\cite{wang2024hypergraph}.  We claim no novelty for
these facts.  We place them at a security boundary: a typed edge generates
candidate and required families, receipts filter the candidate, and an
independent controller product is checked against the admitted family.
Minimal nonfaces become deployment counterexamples only after excluding
outside-support and locally forbidden choices.

\paragraph{Capabilities, linear resources, and consumable authority}
Consumable credentials combine linear authorization with durable ratification
so copied proof material cannot productively reuse bounded authority
~\cite{bowers2007consumable}.  Capability calculi, resource-aware session
types, and capability architectures likewise reason about splitting,
transferring, bounding, delegating, and revoking resources
~\cite{terauchi2006capability,das2018resource,yu2023capstone}.  Such mechanisms
can implement one redemption cell and prevent alias double-spend; we claim none
of those primitives.  They do not exclude a forbidden union of distinct fresh
cells.  Because cell and controller identities transform independently, our
cover theorem checks the remaining higher-order correlation.

\paragraph{Transactions, coordination, and invariant confluence}
Transactional outboxes and stable idempotency keys couple local intent with
eventual delivery and deduplicate retries
~\cite{aws2026outbox,aws2026idempotency}.  Atomix stages and settles agent tool
effects across resource frontiers, while Cordon constructs semantic
transactions over task lineage, state, effects, and delegated authority
~\cite{mohammadi2026atomix,chen2026cordon}.  They are implementation substrates,
not the admission theorem.  Invariant confluence and Complete CALM characterize
when coordination is unnecessary~\cite{bailis2014coordination,
hellerstein2026completecalm}.  We permit coordination and decide whether one
typed edge at a fixed durable prefix has a controller realization between its
required and admitted futures; we claim no universal coordination criterion or
unique finest cover.

\paragraph{Supervisory enforcement and certifying compilation}
Supervisory control synthesizes legal behavior under controllability and
observation assumptions, including partial/relative observation and structured
processes
~\cite{ramadge1987supervisory,cieslak1988partial,cai2015relative,ma2006sts,feng2007ctp}.
Decentralized control is the closest mature account of local controllers
jointly enforcing a specification~\cite{komenda2017decentralized}; security
automata and runtime hyperproperty enforcement mediate trace and cross-trace
policies~\cite{schneider2000enforceable,coenen2022hyperproperties}.  They start
with a plant/specification or policy and study synthesis/enforcement.  We derive
$\mathsf{Required}$ and $\mathsf{Admitted}$ for one online typed edge and the
finite co-liveness evidence its manifest needs.  We claim neither a supervisor
nor a general hyperproperty enforcer.

The artifact follows an untrusted-producer/small-checker pattern
~\cite{necula1997pcc}, but Python is not extracted from Lean and its seal does
not establish $\mathsf{Actual}\subseteq\mathsf{RawPhysical}$.

\paragraph{Agent checkpoint and branching substrates}
ACRFence identifies action replay and authority resurrection after agent
restore and proposes a branch-tagged replay-or-fork check at the tool boundary
~\cite{zheng2026acrfence}.  DART checks when a local checkpoint remains
semantically recoverable after downstream commitments~\cite{yang2026dart}.
Crab, Fork--Explore--Commit, and Agent libOS provide restore, branching, and
checkpoint substrates~\cite{wu2026crab,wang2026fork,zhang2026agentlibos}.  We
instead type six Fork/Restore/Merge edges and check their future against receipts
and controller realization; we claim neither checkpoint fidelity nor an
unmodified runtime adapter.

\paragraph{History-sensitive agent policy and evolving authority}
FORGE specifies Datalog policies over history predicates maintained by an
observability service with an assume/guarantee contract, and a reference
monitor consults those predicates at policy-relevant actions
~\cite{palumbo2026forge}.  It treats general causal history and observation;
we generate one correlated family from a typed edge, filter it by receipts, and
check a co-live controller product.  We claim no policy language or monitor
theorem.

Hu and Wang show that locally benign agent fragments may assemble into harm
that no monitor on the local view can detect~\cite{hu2026localmonitors}.  We do
not claim that general insight.  Our deterministic lower bound says that, for
every $k$, arity-$k$ metadata cannot distinguish two $U(k,k{+}1)$ controller
families with different decisions; the contract computes a sufficient arity.

Earned Authority fixes a transition envelope and effect ceiling when a grant
is issued, distinguishes requested from realized effects, and conditions
authorization continuity on evidence supplied during later agent mutation
~\cite{zhang2026earned}.  We neither replace nor strengthen that ceiling; we
ask which configurations survive a typed edge and whether controller layout
can exceed them.  PORTICO compiles task contracts into epoch-bound capabilities
withdrawn when their episode closes~\cite{santos2026lingering}.  Those handles
can implement local cells; we address correlations among still-valid cells,
not episode closure.

\paragraph{Commit authorization and external effects}
Commit-Time Authorization checks whether one effect witness remains fresh,
causally prior, target-bound, and eligible when the effect commits
~\cite{santos2026committime}.  Ghost Tool Calls shows that an abandoned
speculative call may disclose intent at issue time, before any later commit
decision~\cite{mohammadi2026ghost}.  Atomix and Cordon address transactional
grouping, staging, recovery, and settlement of agent tool effects
~\cite{mohammadi2026atomix,chen2026cordon}.  These boundaries are orthogonal:
fresh, individually valid effects may still be forbidden together.  Our seal
neither authorizes one effect nor prevents issue-time disclosure.

\paragraph{Combined claim boundary}
The novelty is their decision boundary: a typed edge generates required and
candidate futures; receipts select the greatest admitted future; independent
cell/controller normalizations induce a relational product; and a checker emits
positive evidence or a typed minimal failure.  A contract-indexed upper bound
and arbitrary-arity lower family locate when truncated co-liveness evidence is
incomplete.  Under
$\mathsf{Required}\subseteq\mathsf{Actual}
\subseteq\mathsf{RawPhysical}$, the certificate transports to runtime behavior.
