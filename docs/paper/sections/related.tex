\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{History-sensitive authorization}
Abadi and Fournet make access rights depend on execution history and give
guidance for propagating current rights across fork/join; Krukow, Nielsen,
and Sassone use event structures and dynamic checking for history-based policies
~\cite{abadi2003history,krukow2008history}.  FORGE is the closest Agent-era
reference monitor: it evaluates policy over a causal dependency DAG and proves
verdict correctness from sufficient, sound observations
~\cite{palumbo2026forge}.  These systems decide a proposed action from the
history observed so far.  We instead admit a topology-changing operation by
quantifying over its structurally derived future completions, including
logical aliases that advance the new history without a new policy event.
Thus we do not claim the first causal-DAG or fork/join authorization model.

\paragraph{Dynamic controller and property update}
Live controller update is a mature synthesis problem.  Nahabedian et al.
synthesize a sound-and-complete transition controller around hot-swap and
architectural reconfiguration~\cite{nahabedian2020dynamic}.  Amram et al.
compute a bridge while a synthesized GR(1) controller keeps moving, prove
soundness/completeness, and optimize worst-case bridge length
~\cite{amram2022dynamic}.  Bresolin and Lanese construct most-general
property-preserving replacements and characterize safe dynamic state transfer
~\cite{bresolin2021static}.  Transformational supervisor synthesis updates an
old supervisor after a model delta while retaining the result of fresh
synthesis~\cite{thuijsman2022transformational}; reconfigurable DES work also
models explicit configuration events~\cite{macktoobian2022reconfiguration}.

Our result is not a first bridge, state transfer, hot-swap, or maximally
permissive update.  Those abstractions take old/new specifications,
controllers, or transfer relations as input.  Runtime-update models represent
handoff or reconfiguration as semantic behavior, while transformational
synthesis updates the modeled supervisor at design time.  Our starting point
is instead an authenticated Agent history operation.  Its checkpoint lineage
and live topology derive both the new completion contract and every source
owner; its implementation theorem refines the history edge to one irreversible
receipt/occurrence frontier under old-use races.

\paragraph{Automata and supervisory control}
Security automata enforce prefix-closed properties
~\cite{schneider2000enforceable}.  Under full observation, supervisory
control yields supremal controllable behavior
~\cite{ramadge1987supervisory}; partial observation adds observability
conditions~\cite{cieslak1988partial}, and relative observability restores a
union-closed supremal construction~\cite{cai2015relative}.  Residual languages
give canonical deterministic recognizers~\cite{brzozowski1964derivatives}.
Decentralized supervision and localization study correct distribution of a
monolithic result~\cite{komenda2017decentralized,zhang2017localization}.
We use these facts directly.  For a fixed completion family and receipt log,
the greatest-language argument and residual implementation are established
machinery.  The contribution is the Agent-history derivation and the
occurrence-level durable refinement that make those fixed inputs trustworthy.

\paragraph{Causality and rollback}
Event and configuration structures already model causality, conflict, and
concurrency~\cite{winskel1987event,vanglabbeek2009configuration}; our finite
pomset family is intentionally a small contract representation, not a new
concurrency model.  Rollback recovery and continuity systems preserve,
reconstruct, or detect inconsistent past state
~\cite{elnozahy2002rollback,parno2011memoir,matetic2017rote,
brandenburger2017lcm}.  Virtual-machine work observed early that checkpointed
execution becomes a tree and can revive one-time security state
~\cite{garfinkel2005virtual}; ACRFence identifies action replay and authority
resurrection in Agent Restore~\cite{zheng2026acrfence}.  History admission
does not make the world rollbackable.  It retains discarded nodes as
provenance and decides which future can coexist with their durable effects.

\paragraph{Durable effects and fencing}
Consumable credentials provide atomic distributed ratification and accounting
of bounded authority~\cite{bowers2007consumable}.  Commit-Time Authorization checks
freshness, causal priority, effect binding, and eligibility when an Agent
effect becomes durable~\cite{santos2026committime}; PORTICO uses
task-local epochs and stale-handle rejection~\cite{santos2026lingering}.
Transactional outboxes and stable idempotency keys can provide the durable
release substrate~\cite{aws2026outbox,aws2026idempotency}.  We assume these
per-effect mechanisms.  The new obligation is cut-wide: derive and close
every owner whose continuation the Agent edit changes, while ordering both
new receipts and receipt-silent aliases against target activation.
