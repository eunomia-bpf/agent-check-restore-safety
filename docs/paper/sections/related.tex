\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{History-sensitive authorization}
History-based access control and event-structure monitors decide an action
from its observed past~\cite{abadi2003history,krukow2008history}; FORGE proves
Agent verdict correctness from sufficient causal-DAG
observations~\cite{palumbo2026forge}.  Agent history admission instead judges
a topology-changing edit: every raw-compatible outcome must retain a safe
completion after every prefix, including receipt-silent aliases.

\paragraph{Reversal, checkpoints, and update}
Reversible session calculi provide named checkpoints, causally consistent
rollback, and session fidelity; session programming adds typed
commit/rollback/abort~\cite{dezani2016checkpoints,castellani2017concurrent,
mezzina2021causal,mezzina2025checkpoint}.  Retractable contracts model
backtracking and speculative alternatives~\cite{barbanera2018contracts}.
Dynamic-update and property-update theories synthesize controllers, bridges,
or most-general replacements from given old/new specifications
~\cite{nahabedian2020dynamic,amram2022dynamic,bresolin2021static,
thuijsman2022transformational,macktoobian2022reconfiguration}.  Here the edit
schema must derive inherited obligations, world receipts remain irreversible,
and the greatest fixed point decides whether those obligations survive every
compatible prefix.

\paragraph{Automata and causality}
Security automata enforce prefix-closed properties
~\cite{schneider2000enforceable}; supervisory control constructs supremal
behavior under observation assumptions
~\cite{ramadge1987supervisory,cieslak1988partial,cai2015relative}.
Residual languages yield canonical recognizers, while decentralized
supervision studies distribution
~\cite{brzozowski1964derivatives,komenda2017decentralized,
zhang2017localization}.  Our operator instead couples indexed completions:
removing one schedule because it strands another outcome may recursively
invalidate earlier prefixes.  Pomsets are our finite representation of
causality, conflict, and concurrency
~\cite{winskel1987event,vanglabbeek2009configuration}.

\paragraph{Rollback continuity and durable effects}
Rollback systems preserve or detect past state
~\cite{elnozahy2002rollback,parno2011memoir,matetic2017rote,
brandenburger2017lcm}; VM work identified checkpoint trees that revive
one-time state~\cite{garfinkel2005virtual}, and ACRFence targets authority
resurrection in Agent Restore~\cite{zheng2026acrfence}.  Consumable
credentials, commit-time checks, stale-handle fencing, outboxes, and stable
idempotency keys protect individual effects
~\cite{bowers2007consumable,santos2026committime,santos2026lingering,
aws2026outbox,aws2026idempotency}.  We assume these mechanisms and add the
history boundary: a domain-wide cut persists the schema-derived edit and
orders both new receipts and alias progress before activating its monitor.
