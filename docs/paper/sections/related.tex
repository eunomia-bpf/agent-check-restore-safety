\section{Related Work}
% 相关工作
\label{sec:related}

Rebound, ACRFence, DART, and Atomix address rollback, fencing, restore admission, and settlement~\cite{burke2026rebound,zheng2026acrfence,yang2026dart,mohammadi2026atomix}, but none derives six-edit obligations from the outcome--occurrence--cell--receipt relation or returns greatest realizations or impossibility proofs.
% Rebound、ACRFence、DART 与 Atomix 分别处理回滚、隔离、恢复准入与结算~\cite{burke2026rebound,zheng2026acrfence,yang2026dart,mohammadi2026atomix}，但它们都不从结果--发生项--单元--回执关系推导六种编辑义务，也不返回最大实现或不可能性证明。
Prior synthesis and enforcement start from supplied control instances~\cite{dequeiroz2005multitasking,malik2008generalised,finkbeiner2022live,aceto2018suppressions}; our theorem derives Agent-history obligations and spans checked admission to epoch realization.
