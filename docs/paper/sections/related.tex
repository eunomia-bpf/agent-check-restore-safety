\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{History-sensitive authorization}
History-based access control and event-structure monitors judge a next action
from its past~\cite{abadi2003history,krukow2008history}.  FORGE proves Agent
verdicts from causal-DAG observations~\cite{palumbo2026forge}.  We judge a
topology-changing edit: every contract-compatible outcome must retain a safe
completion after every admitted prefix, including receipt-silent \Alias occurrences.

\paragraph{Reversal and update}
Reversible and checkpointed sessions guarantee rollback consistency and typed
progress~\cite{dezani2016checkpoints,castellani2017concurrent,
mezzina2021causal,mezzina2025checkpoint}.  Retractable contracts model
backtracking~\cite{barbanera2018contracts}.  Update theories synthesize
controllers or bridges between given specifications
~\cite{nahabedian2020dynamic,amram2022dynamic,bresolin2021static,
thuijsman2022transformational,macktoobian2022reconfiguration}.  Our edit
schemas instead derive inherited obligations while receipts remain
irreversible, and admission preserves those obligations after every admitted
prefix.

\paragraph{Automata and causality}
Security automata enforce prefix-closed properties
~\cite{schneider2000enforceable}, while supervisory control constructs
supremal legal behavior~\cite{ramadge1987supervisory}.  Multitasking and
generalized nonblocking preserve multiple conditionally marked goals
~\cite{dequeiroz2005multitasking,malik2008generalised}.
\Cref{prop:outcome-necessary} makes the relationship exact: with
outcome-compatibility markings, our kernel is a conditional
generalized-nonblocking computation.  Without them, projected marker
nonblocking is strictly weaker.  Our history-admission construction derives
the markings from authenticated Fork/Restore/Merge topology, schema-authorized retirement,
and irreversible receipts, then realizes the greatest behavior through a
durable history cut.  Derivatives provide the canonical recognizer
~\cite{brzozowski1964derivatives}.  Pomsets represent causality and concurrency
~\cite{winskel1987event,vanglabbeek2009configuration}.

\paragraph{Durable effects}
Rollback systems preserve or detect past state
~\cite{elnozahy2002rollback,parno2011memoir,matetic2017rote,
brandenburger2017lcm}, and checkpoint trees can revive one-time state
~\cite{garfinkel2005virtual}.  ACRFence targets authority resurrection in
Agent Restore~\cite{zheng2026acrfence}.  Consumable credentials, commit-time
checks, stale-handle fencing, outboxes, and idempotency protect individual
effects~\cite{bowers2007consumable,santos2026committime,
santos2026lingering,aws2026outbox,aws2026idempotency}.  Our theory instead
characterizes whole history edits and orders receipts and \Alias progress
before monitor activation.
