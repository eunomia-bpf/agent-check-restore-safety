\section{Related Work}
% 相关工作
\label{sec:related}

Output-commit recovery delays effects until state commits and reconstructs a fixed computation after failure; it does not admit topology-changing Agent edits under indexed outcomes and shared authority~\cite{strom1985optimistic,elnozahy2002rollback}.
% 输出提交恢复将效果延迟到状态提交后，并在故障后重建固定计算；它不对带索引结果与共享权限下改变拓扑的 Agent 编辑作准入判定~\cite{strom1985optimistic,elnozahy2002rollback}。

Generalized-nonblocking and Live Synthesis consume supplied plants/markings or old specifications/executions; ours derives six-edit obligations and necessary-and-sufficient admission state from authenticated Agent history before greatest installation~\cite{dequeiroz2005multitasking,malik2008generalised,finkbeiner2022live}.
% 广义非阻塞与 Live Synthesis 使用给定的被控对象/标记或旧规范/执行；我们的理论则在最大安装之前，从经认证的 Agent 历史派生六类编辑义务以及必要且充分的准入状态~\cite{dequeiroz2005multitasking,malik2008generalised,finkbeiner2022live}。

ACRFence/DART/Atomix/Rebound fence post-Restore replay, choose preserving checkpoints, settle after frontiers, or make rollback atomic and auditable, but take supplied logs, scopes, classes, frontiers, or policies~\cite{zheng2026acrfence,yang2026dart,mohammadi2026atomix,burke2026rebound}.
% ACRFence/DART/Atomix/Rebound 隔离 Restore 后的重放、选择保持既有工作的检查点、在前沿之后结算，或使回滚具有原子性与可审计性，但它们使用给定的日志、范围、类别、前沿或策略~\cite{zheng2026acrfence,yang2026dart,mohammadi2026atomix,burke2026rebound}。
