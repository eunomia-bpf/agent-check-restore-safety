# Experiment Plan: RQ2 operational promotion boundary

## Research Question

- RQ exactly as written in the paper: **RQ2: What changes when a conditional effect becomes durable?** Can exact promotion remain inside the natural choice/parallel policy language, and when does algebraic repair correspond to an executable ordering of effects?
- Specific uncertainty tested here: whether Boundary II is a genuine operational characterization caused by coupling conditional-to-durable resource promotion with irreversible owner cleanup, or merely a direct restatement of configuration-family intersection, generic resource accounting, or supervisory enabledness. Concretely, under a fixed valid batch, exact cumulative prefix repair, no interleaved lifecycle mutation, and immediate deterministic cleanup, test the claimed equivalence between (i) every permutation of nonempty owner groups remaining executable and reaching the atomic result and (ii) every promoted owner having a witness in the final repaired support.
- Why the answer matters: this iff is the strongest current RQ2 theorem and the proposed basis of the industrial “serial or seal” rule. If its proof uses only an already-published lemma under renaming, the theory-led CSF story must be demoted or rebuilt. If it survives an explicit closest-model reduction attempt, kernel mechanization, and assumption-independence attack, it supplies a precise lifecycle principle rather than an intuition or bounded example.

## Paper-Value Admission

- Planned role: decisive.
- Largest credible paper story this experiment could unlock: an exact domain-specific characterization of contextual transition independence for conditional-to-durable authority: one final-support query decides whether all owner-group interleavings preserve enabledness and converge, explaining exactly when an agent runtime may dispatch in arbitrary order versus requiring a verified order or atomic sealing.
- Strongest reviewer reject argument or load-bearing uncertainty addressed: after subtracting known configuration structures, the established partial-order-reduction definition of independence (enabledness preservation plus commutation), quantitative resource semantics, linear/consumable credentials, and maximally permissive supervisory control, Boundary II may collapse to an elementary instance and therefore fail theorem-level novelty.
- Independent evidence added beyond existing runs and published results: the current evidence is a prose proof and two bounded Python witnesses. This experiment adds (1) an explicit source-to-source semantic map against the closest primary literature, (2) a general Lean theorem over arbitrary finite owners/configurations/weights rather than fixed examples, (3) kernel-checked positive and negative directions, and (4) countermodels showing which stated assumptions are indispensable.
- Why the result is not tautological, already settled, or dominated: the result is rejected as novel if the formal map derives it directly from a cited theorem. Otherwise the mechanization must derive order-wide operational enabledness and final-state equality from weaker filtration laws, and the mutation suite must demonstrate that removing self-neutrality, exact cumulative repair, or irreversible support cleanup changes the result. Both outcomes are belief-changing. A mere encoding of the iff as a premise is forbidden.
- Paper decision if positive: center RQ2 on the complete final-support characterization of contextual independence for authority promotion; explicitly credit generic independence/commutation to partial-order reduction and configuration semantics; present the new claim only as the one-final-state specialization plus its serial-or-seal runtime consequence.
- Paper decision if contradictory, mixed, or inconclusive: a direct inherited derivation demotes Boundary II and triggers a search for a stronger existential-scheduling or minimal-coordination theorem; a false direction or counterexample requires correcting the model and paper; a theorem that survives only under stronger assumptions narrows the claim and makes those assumptions explicit; incomplete source mapping or incomplete mechanization yields no novelty claim.
- Best alternative experiment and why this one has higher decision value: a StateFork/Waypoint protected-sink crash study would strengthen the systems instantiation but cannot repair a theorem-novelty reject; a larger public-trace scan is redundant because the completed TraceLab audit already establishes workload relevance and the missing observability fields. The present experiment directly attacks the sole theory-led CSF blocker.

## Expected And Alternative Outcomes

- Current expected answer: the theorem holds and generalizes to a small support-erasing commutative-action calculus, but the generic target notion is already transition independence: enabledness preservation plus commutation. The possible new result is narrower and sharper—under authority promotion and immediate cleanup, final-owner support is a necessary-and-sufficient one-final-state test for that otherwise prefix-quantified property.
- Strongest competing explanation: once owner groups are represented as events and exact repair as monotone configuration filtering, final support is an immediate specialization of asynchronous-step or independence definitions; the authority vocabulary adds no new derivation, decision procedure, or proof obligation.
- Result that would contradict the expectation: a cited theorem plus a definition-preserving translation proves the same iff without adding lifecycle-specific operational state; Lean exposes the result as definitional after importing only generic antitonicity/support facts; either implication fails in a finite well-formed authority state; or final-state equality requires an unstated normalization/cleanup premise.

## Comparison

- Proposed system or method: Boundary II as an instantiated theorem of exact cumulative promotion plus immediate support cleanup, proved without assuming universal serialization or target equality.
- Main baselines and the competing position each represents: (1) configuration structures, representing the claim that the asynchronous-step condition—every intermediate set is a configuration—already implies the result; (2) partial-order reduction, representing the claim that enabledness preservation plus commutation already is the whole theorem; (3) resource/supervisory authorization semantics, representing the claim that quantitative consumption, atomic ratification, maximally permissive enabledness, and guard synthesis already derive the same final-support test.
- Why each main baseline needs a matched run instead of citation alone: no numerical rerun is appropriate. Each baseline receives a matched formal translation attempt on the same owner-group promotion instance. Citation alone cannot decide whether its state, step, support, and resource operators preserve the theorem's premises and conclusion. A successful derivation is a baseline win; a missing operator is recorded rather than filled silently with the proposed semantics.
- Controls or ablations, labeled separately: remove or mutate exact cumulative repair, antitonicity, self-neutrality of an owner's own promotion on owner-containing configurations, immediate cleanup, cleanup irreversibility, fixed batch/owner partition, and no-interleaving. Each mutation needs either a smallest finite countermodel or an explanation that it is derivable from the remaining assumptions.
- Conclusion if each main baseline matches or wins: a definition-preserving configuration-structure derivation removes theorem-level novelty; a partial-order result that yields final support without a new authority-specific lemma reduces Boundary II to a renamed independence test; a resource/supervisor derivation makes it an application. In each case the paper must cite and demote the iff rather than relabel it.
- Information, tuning, and compute fairness: proposed and baseline interpretations use the same source family, owner map, batch partition, weights, final support, enabledness observation, and equality notion. The comparison may not grant a baseline the paper's cleanup or conditional-to-durable operator without labeling that operator as added glue.
- Split or leakage rule when relevant: theorem statements and expected outcomes are frozen in this plan before implementation. Positive examples, negative examples, and assumption mutations are separate declarations; no countermodel is excluded because it disproves the expected theorem.

## Interpretation

- Positive result: the iff and authority instantiation kernel-check, the assumption audit shows a nontrivial minimal operational package, and no baseline supplies the cleanup-coupled result without importing that package. The paper may claim a new general operational boundary and its agent-runtime consequence, while crediting all configuration/resource/supervisor ingredients.
- Negative or contradictory result: a closest model derives the iff directly, or Lean finds a valid-state counterexample. Demote or remove Boundary II as a contribution and redirect RQ2 toward existential schedule synthesis/minimal coordination or a stronger lifecycle refinement result.
- Mixed or inconclusive result: if only the generic theorem holds but the authority instantiation needs stronger premises, report the narrower theorem and expose those premises; if the theorem holds but novelty mapping remains ambiguous, do not claim novelty and continue the primary-source audit; if only finite examples compile, treat the run as dependency evidence, not a result.
- Target paper figure or table: one compact three-column theorem-separation table (published ingredient / added operational ingredient / resulting conclusion) plus a small scheduler decision diagram: factor locally, otherwise arbitrary-order serial if final support holds, otherwise verify an order or seal atomically. No performance plot is implied.
